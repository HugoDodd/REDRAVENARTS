Abdul Karim Al-Orrayed

B. 1934

Artist Biography

Abdul Karim Al-Orrayed is a Bahraini artist who works in watercolour, drawings, oils and prints. Although his style has remained based in realism, Al-Orrayed was a genuine pioneer in Bahrain. Along with Rashid Oraifi and Hussein Al-Sini he co-founded in the 1950s the Art Amateurs Association and later the Bahrain Contemporary Arts Association. In 1960 Al-Orrayed opened in Manama the first commercial art gallery in Bahrain in order to sell his paintings. A mainstay of Arts Association exhibitions over the years, his work was shown on numerous occasions internationally to highlight Bahraini art. These included Arab art exhibitions in Syria in 1975, Morocco 1976, Algeria 1977, Singapore 1981, Jordan 1983, and Egypt and Oman in 1984. More recently his work was included in 2022 in a major exhibition in Bahrain Art Gallery ‘Khalid Modern: Pioneers and collectives in the Arab Peninsula 1941-2008’. In 2024 his work was included in a travelling exhibition of Bahraini art to Baku, Azerbaijan. The Bahrain Arts Centre holds works by Al-Orrayed in its permanent collection.
