Albert Osabu Bartimeus

1927–1988

Artist Biography

Albert O. Bartimeus is considered to be one of the founders of modern Ghanaian art. He trained at Kumasi College of Art (which became Kwame Nkrumah University on independence) and went on to become the first president of the Ghana Association of Artists. A prize named in his honour is given each year to a young Ghanaian artist. Bartimeus was forced to take a government post to make a living but painted throughout his life; he had an exuberant style, both in terms of palette and use of paint, concentrating on landscapes, seascapes and depictions of Ghanaian people. The National Museum in Accra held a retrospective in 1996, and in 2019 a major exhibition at the Dei Gallery in Accra showed his work alongside that of Owusu Dartey and Edmund Tetteh as the three ‘undisputed’ fathers of modern Ghanaian art. Bartimeus’ work is held in the National Gallery and the National Museums in Accra. It is also in the Nubuke Foundation collection and the Museum of African Art in Belgrade, Serbia.
