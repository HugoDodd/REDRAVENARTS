Atsiagbekor dancers

Albert Osabu Bartimeus

1967

Oil on board

79 × 61 cm

Signed, dated 1967. Titled on part-label verso, where location given as Khoforidua.

Another label gives details of Bartimeus’ training and position as Eastern Region director of the Institute of Arts and Culture. Atsiagbekor is a traditional warrior dance of the Ewe people. Ghana
