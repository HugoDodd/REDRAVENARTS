Bruce Onobrakpeya

B. 1932

Artist Biography

Bruce Onobrakpeya is an Nigerian artist famed for constant technical innovations in his printing techniques. He attended art school in Zaria in 1957, later joining Uche Okeke’s Zaria Art Society which proved more influential than his formal Western art training. Onobrakpeya’s work began to feature elongated figures, ignored perspective and invoked the supernatural through ambiguous decoration. He became an increasingly influential figure driving a move towards a more modern contemporary art scene in Nigeria and exhibited widely internationally. His work is held by public collections across Nigeria, including the National Gallery of Modern Art in Lagos, and internationally in the United States – the Smithsonian in Washington and Museum of African and African American Art in Buffalo New York - Canada, Finland, Iraq and elsewhere. Ononrakpeya’s paintings and prints were included in the major exhibition African Modernism in America 1947-67, held in 2023-24 at the Phillips Collection in Washington, United States and at Nigerian Modernism at the Tate Modern in London 2025-26.
