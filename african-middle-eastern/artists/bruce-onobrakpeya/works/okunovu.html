Okunovu

Bruce Onobrakpeya

1970

Deep etching

59 × 45 cm

Signed, titled, medium, dated January 1970 and numbered 23/30 all in pencil below image recto. Signed and titled in the etching too.

Sheet size , image . Okunovu is a communal shrine to the primordial divinity of the Idjerhe people in Benin, which fascinated Onobrakpeya and to which he returned artistically as a theme. Other size 79x57cm (H x W).
