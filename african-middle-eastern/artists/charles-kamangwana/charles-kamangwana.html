Charles Kamangwana

1972–2015

Artist Biography

Charles Kamangwana was born in Mutoko, Matabeleland and attended the BAT Workshop, National Gallery of Zimbabwe (now the Zimbabwe School of Visual Art and Design) from 1992-1994. Whilst still studying his work was included in the annual Zimbabwean Heritage exhibitions and he won awards for photography, sculpture and painting. Kamangwana went on to have numerous solo shows and mixed exhibitions in Zimbabwe but also had his work included in shows in the UK, Netherlands, Germany, Italy and South Africa. His paintings, largely in an expressionist style, were known for sympathetic portrayals of ordinary people but he also participated in ‘Prominent Personalities: Portraits of Zimbabweans’ at the National Gallery in 2015, the year of his untimely death. Kamangwana worked as a teacher and lecturer as well as an artist and was a co-founder and chair of African Colours Artists’ Association (AAA) which was set up in 2002.
