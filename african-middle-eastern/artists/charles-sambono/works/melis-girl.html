Meli’s Girl

Charles Sambono

Oil on paper

56 × 76 cm

Signed.

Jasper Marsh at Hanson’s said the Sambono paintings came from an extremely solid aristocratic source – indeed, the family that in the 1980s sold their huge house in Mayfair to Saudi Arabia for its Embassy. All appear to be ex-Westbourne Gallery. Westbourne Gallery was a pioneering London gallery (1970s+ ?) showing contemporary African as well as Caribbean and Latin American works. It was apparently founded by a Ghanaian ex-boxer called Sonny McNorthy and later managed by Donna Thompson-Ray.

Westbourne Gallery label gives title and asking price of £900. Title and No. 30 also marked verso. Zambia
