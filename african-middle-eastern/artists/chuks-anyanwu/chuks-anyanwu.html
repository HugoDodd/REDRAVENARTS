Chuks Anyanwu

1937–c.1990

Artist Biography

Chuks Anyanwu was born in Eastern Nigeria in 1937 and initially studied art and design at the Yaba Technical Institute. In 1959, he attended the London Art College, and in 1960 he was awarded a Federal scholarship to study Fine Art at the Nigerian College of Arts, Science and Technology (later Ahmadu Bello University) in Zaria.

Throughout his career, Anyanwu earned numerous awards for his art, exhibiting widely while also working professionally as an art editor, book illustrator, and cartoonist for major newspapers across Nigeria and Ghana. His solo exhibitions included a prominent showing at The Senior Staff Club at the University of Ibadan in May 1973.

Anyanwu's distinctive stylized compositions elegantly fused traditional folkloric themes with modernist geometric structures. A 1982 mask painting by the artist is held in the permanent collection of the National Gallery of Art in Nigeria.
