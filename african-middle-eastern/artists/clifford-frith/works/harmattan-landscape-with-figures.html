Harmattan landscape with Figures

Clifford Frith

1960–1961

Oil on canvas

61 x 122 cm

Signed; gallery/exhibition label verso inscribed with title, 'Zaria Art School 1960/61', and catalogue number 8

Provenance & Literature: Formerly in the collection of Grant Waters. Illustrated on page 122 (fig. 3.27) in Chika Okeke-Agulu’s Post-Colonial Modernism: Art and Decolonization in Twentieth-Century Nigeria (2015), where Okeke-Agulu discusses the work as an example of Frith experimenting with an abstract pictorial language. The title refers to the Harmattan, a dry, dusty trade wind that blows across West Africa during the winter season.
