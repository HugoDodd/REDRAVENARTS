Edward Njenga

1922–2022

Artist Biography

Edward Samuel Njenga was born in Ngecha in the Kiambu district of Kenya. Although officially recorded as born in 1922, he noted in an interview that his birth was around 1928, while other sources cite 1932. Njenga’s mother was a potter, but following a two-year period held in a colonial detention camp, he trained as a social worker and pursued that profession throughout his life while creating art—primarily sculpture—in his spare time. Njenga was largely self-taught and began sculpting in the 1960s, inspired by time spent at a UK art college while studying social work in England in 1963. His early subjects focused on poorer Kenyans in daily life, establishing a strong social realist approach that defined his career.

His initial works were sold at the New Stanley Hotel Art Gallery, and Njenga went on to participate in numerous exhibitions across Kenya and abroad, including in Germany, where a scholarship enabled him to study at the Hanover School of Fine Arts from 1971 to 1972. A prominent sculpture by Njenga stands outside the United Nations Headquarters in Nairobi, and in 2013, the Nairobi National Museum presented a major retrospective of his work entitled A Son’s Dedication to celebrate 50 years of Kenyan independence.
