Emna Zghal

b.1970

Artist Biography

Emna Zghal was born in Tunisia and received her artistic training there and in the United States, where she has lived in recent decades. A noted poet and academic, in addition to her painting, Zghal is best known for her images of vast imagined spaces, mixing the abstract expressionist and the surreal. Zghal had her first solo exhibition in Marburg, Germany in 1993 and this was followed by others in Tunisia in 1994 and 1996, the Cite Internationale de Arts, Paris in 1995, the Tunisian Embassy in Washington in 1999, the Scene Gallery, New York in 2002, Galerie El Marsa, Tunis in 2004 and thereafter largely in the United States. Her work has also been shown in numerous mixed exhibitions in the United States and elsewhere, including Japan, France, Italy, Algeria and the United Arab Emirates. Highlights include the New Delhi Triennial, India in 1997, Abstract Expressionism Then & Now at the Flint Institute of Art, USA in 2012 and Maghreb: Works from North Africa, Doha, Qatar in 2022. US public institutions that hold her work include Newark Museum and the Africa Center, New York.
