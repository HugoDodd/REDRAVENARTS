Untitled

Faustin Kitsiba

Oil on board

46 × 60 cm

Signed.

African mask. Marked verso Kitsiba, Dakar. Marking verso suggests painted in Senegal. This could be linked to the 1966 First World Festival of Black Arts in Dakar, at which Kitsiba exhibited alongside fellow Congolese painters Marcel Gotene and Guy Leon Fylla. The strong subject matter certainly fits in with that exhibition’s theme of Negritude.
