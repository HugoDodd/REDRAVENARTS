Fela Odaranile

B. 1949

Artist Biography

Fela Odaranile was born in Abeokuta, Ogun State in Nigeria and attended the Oshogbo workshops in 1964 and 1965 under Dennis Williams and Georgina Beier. He then studied at Ahmad Bello University and at the Ori-Olokun print workshop at Ife-Ife under Prof S Irein Wangboje. He later worked at the Ogun-Timehin Workshop with Rufus Ogundele and others, also in Ife-Ife. A major Oshogbo school artist, Odaranile had a solo exhibition in 1974 at the National Museum in Lagos and a second there in 1977. He also had one-man exhibitions at the Italian Cultural Institute and the Goethe Institute in Lagos in the 1970s. His work was part of many group shows in Nigeria, notably the FESTEC ’77 exhibition in 1977, but was also shown abroad in Germany and Austria in the late 1970s and early 1980s and the UK, USA, Canada and Australia later in the 1980s and early 1990s. Highlights included the Commonwealth Institute’s show on Oshogbo Art in 1985 in London and 1986 in Edinburgh. The National Gallery of Modern Art in Lagos holds his work, as do public collections in Germany.
