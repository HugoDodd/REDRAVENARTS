Fidel Oyiogu

B. 1957

Artist Biography

Fidel Nnamdi Oyiogu is a Nigerian artist the bulk of whose work reflects his native Uli traditions, particularly in his use of vibrant colours. He trained at the Institute of Management and Technology at Enugu from 1980-1985 and then worked as a cartoonist for the Champion Newspaper in the 1980s. He gained increasing recognition for his paintings and in the later 1980s was included in group shows in the African Art Gallery in Germany and at the Goethe Institute in Lagos. Living and working in Lagos, Oyiogu had a major solo show in 2012 at Terra Kulture on Victoria Island titled Woman Forms and Desires. In 2013 he then had a touring exhibition in Germany, Austria and the Netherlands. He has been a mainly figurative artist for the bulk of his career but in 2014 Oyiogu turned to more abstract forms of art, achieving continued success.
