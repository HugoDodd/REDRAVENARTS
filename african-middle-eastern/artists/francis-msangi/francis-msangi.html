Francis Msangi

1937–2003

Artist Biography

Born in Usangi, Tanzania, Francis Msangi (sometimes recorded as K. Francis Msangi or Musangi) attended Makerere University in Kampala, Uganda from 1959 to 1964. He later taught art extensively, including positions at the University of Nairobi in Kenya from 1968 to 1972 and the California College of Arts and Crafts starting in 1973. Msangi won the Margaret Trowell Award in 1963 and became renowned for his graceful line drawings, abstract lithographs, and oil paintings. He exhibited widely across Tanzania and East Africa, with his work also featured in international exhibitions in New York, Germany, and Poland throughout the late 1960s and early 1970s.
