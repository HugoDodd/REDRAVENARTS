Francois Iloki

1934–1993

Artist Biography

Francois Iloki, along with Francois Thango and a few others, was a leading light of the Poto Poto paintings school set up by Pierre Lods in Brazzaville, capital of the Republic of Congo, in the early 1950s. Iloki, father of the artist Jaques Iloki, had work exhibited along with other members of the Poto Poto school as far afield as Paris, South Africa and at the Museum of Modern Art in New York. His paintings are in the permanent collection of the Poto Poto school museum in Brazzaville.
