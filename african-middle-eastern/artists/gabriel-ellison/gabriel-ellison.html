Gabriel Ellison

1930–2017

Artist Biography

Gabriel Ellison was born in Lusaka and was to spend the bulk of her life in Zambia before ending her days in South Africa. She was educated in both Northern and Southern Rhodesia but learnt her art via private tuition in Britain. Ellison was head of the Graphic Art section of the Zambian Ministry of Information and produced designs for numerous sets of stamps from the 1960s-1980s, but she is probably best known for designing the Zambian national flag in 1964. In addition to her art and design work Ellison wrote novels, many factual works on the local flora and fauna and in 2004 wrote a book on Zambian art history. She was an early member of the Lusaka Art Society and later a member of the Royal Society of Arts. Her work was shown in numerous mixed exhibitions including at Mpapa Gallery, the first commercial gallery in the country, and in 2014 at the Jubilee exhibition at the Livingstone Art Gallery celebrating 50 years of art in an independent Zambia. Ellison’s murals decorate Barclay’s Bank on Cairo Road in Lusaka and Kenneth Kaunda International Airport, whilst the Zambian government awarded her the honour Grand Officer of Distinguished Service.
