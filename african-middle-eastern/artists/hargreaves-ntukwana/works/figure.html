Figure

Hargreaves Ntukwana

1977

Oil on canvas board

55x95 cm

signed, dated 13/7/77

Provenance: Verso is numbered 2567 and letters 1dKGi tloiled. Purchased Bonhams K 2/11. Unsold at African sales Bonhams K Oct’10 £500-700 and South African sale Bonhams NBS 3/10 £1000-1500. Collection of Dr John Hofmeyr, a South African physician who in 1957 became District Surveyor and Mission Doctor to the Benedictine Hospital, Nkandhla, Central Zululand.
