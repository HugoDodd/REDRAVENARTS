Artist Biography

Hayder (or Haydar) Idris Ibrahim is a Sudanese artist who paints in a similar style to Ibrahim el-Salahi and for whom calligraphy and Sufism are integral parts of his work. His paintings have been shown on Sudan Artists Gallery, an on-line museum documenting Sudanese artists, and he is mentioned as being a painter working abroad on the Sudanese Contemporary Art website. In August 2010 the Rashid Diab Arts Centre in Khartoum, Sudan gave Idris a solo show titled “Exhibition of Select Calligraphy – acrylic on canvas – by Haydar Idris”.
