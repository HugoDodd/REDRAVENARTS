Hubert Nii Abbey

b.c.1955

Artist Biography

Hubert Nii Abbey graduated in Graphic Design from the Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST) at Kumasi, Ghana in 1977. He taught fine art in Ghana and Nigeria at secondary schools and technical colleges and mounted several exhibitions in London in the mid-1980s, showing scraperboards and linocuts. The exhibitions included Westbourne Gallery and in 1984 at the Africa Centre in Covent Garden. He also wrote a number of books, including one on the artist Kofi Antubam.
