Ashok

Hubert Nii Abbey

Acrylic on board (cryla)

66 × 38 cm

Not signed.

Title and medium verso. Label verso for Ann Twentyman, Great Western Road. Also James Bourlet shipping label. Cryla was an acrylic paint used in the 1960s. Twentyman was a Cambridge artist who charged quite hefty prices – a 1969 work for 200 guineas. Attended Ely School for Girls. A portrait of Twentyman in 1962 by Jaspar Rose forms part of a large collection of such portraits done by Rose in later years.
