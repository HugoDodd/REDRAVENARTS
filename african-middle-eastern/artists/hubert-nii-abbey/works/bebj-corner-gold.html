Bebj corner (gold)

Hubert Nii Abbey

1984

Print/mixed media

65 × 54 cm

Signed and dated ’84. Signed again, indistinctly titled verso where Deigan writes ‘no edition number’ so presumably a one-off.

Javanese door. Windibank 7/22. these two prints/mm have the same title but are different images. One of the images is the same base as a work in the Fry Gallery Collection in Saffron Waldon which is titled Javanese door.
