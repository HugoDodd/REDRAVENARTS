Blue and White

Hubert Nii Abbey

1962

Ink on paper

71 × 55 cm

Initialled verso where dated May ’62 and marked London. Arthur Tooth label on backboard, where titled and given stock number 6699D.

Exhibited at Kelly’s solo show at Arthur Tooth’s in 1962.

Separate label gives artist name and date May 1962. light clean 6/19. + frame refurb this painting can be seen on video taken of the show and there is a still of it as fig. 185 in Tricia Y Paik’s book ‘Ellsworth Kelly’ (Phaidon 2015). Main question is whether there has been any fading, given the title. Toovey’s said the painting came from a deceased estate in London – indeed it was the only painting, the other items being furniture etc. Frame has Dr Shanks marked on it.
