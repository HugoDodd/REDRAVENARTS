Enterro indigene em Angola

Hubert Nii Abbey

1962

Oil on canvas

98 × 78 cm

Indistinctly signed $NSOYO and dated ’62/67.

Label verso titles the painting, which translates as native funeral, and says it was from the collection of Maria Helena B. Da Veiga (presumably Condessa Maria Helena Beirao da Veiga). Sticker for Dr V M Wadsworth verso. Dr Vivian Michael Wadsworth was an agricultural economist who was Chief Economist at the Federal Ministry of Agriculture in the Federation of Rhodesia & Nyasaland in the late 1950s before undertaking a business career. He was Managing Director from 1963-68 of a food company Fabrica Nacional de Margarina (SARL) in Lisbon and was also a Director of Benguela Railway Co., the owner and operator of Angolan railways. Later he was Chairman of Belfast shipbuilders Harland & Wolff.
