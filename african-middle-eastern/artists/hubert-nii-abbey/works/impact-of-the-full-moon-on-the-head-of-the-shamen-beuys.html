Impact of the full moon on the head of the shamen Beuys

Hubert Nii Abbey

1 × 1 cm

Attributed: listed in the stock document as "Beuys, Joseph (attributed)". Painted in the style of Zwei Frauen 1955 by Beuys (sold Christies Amsterdam April 2014). in mixed lot in dispersal of works from Denis Bowen’s estate The lot included mostly Bowens but also a Scottie Wilson. Christies said the writing looked to be that of Beuys (indeed it does) but would need verification by Galerie Thaddeus Roppac which runs the estate. This gallery in February 2020 said they could no longer offer verification due to the legal disputes that had arisen from such judgements.
