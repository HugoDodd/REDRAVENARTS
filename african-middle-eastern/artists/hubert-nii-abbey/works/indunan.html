Indunan

Hubert Nii Abbey

1960

Bronze plus its clay model

11 × 1 cm

Both signed, Hezekiel Ntuli, Eshowe. Bronze dated 1960 and titled on the plaque.

Collection of Dr John Hofmeyr, a South African physician who in 1957 became District Surveyor and Mission Doctor to the Benedictine Hospital, Nkandhla, Central Zululand.

Unsigned stone carving of monkey and pot. 11cm high, 20cm long. Same provenance.
