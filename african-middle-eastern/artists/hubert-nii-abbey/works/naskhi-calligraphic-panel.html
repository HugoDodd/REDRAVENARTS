Naskhi calligraphic panel

Hubert Nii Abbey

1910

Gouache on card

27 × 17 cm

It is dated 1328, which equates to 1910 in the Western calendar.

Translated by Matthew Elliot as “There is no (strong) youth but Ali and there is no sword but Zulfiqar”. Zulfiqar was Ali’s famous sword and the text is written in Shii.
