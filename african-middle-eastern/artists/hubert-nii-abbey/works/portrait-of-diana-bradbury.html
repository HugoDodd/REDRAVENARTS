Portrait of Diana Bradbury

Hubert Nii Abbey

1962

Pastel

47 × 31 cm

Signed and dated ’62.

Title given by auction house based on vendor’s description, presumably with a family link to Bradbury. Diana Bradbury, who was to become an actress, was 20 at the time of this portrait and the painting was likely number 48 in the April 1962 Qantas Gallery exhibition – ‘Young Girl’ priced at 35 guineas.
