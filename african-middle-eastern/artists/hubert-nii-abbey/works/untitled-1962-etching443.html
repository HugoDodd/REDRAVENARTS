Untitled

Hubert Nii Abbey

1962

Etching

29 × 36 cm

Signed, dated ’62, marked E.C. (artist’s proof?).

Donkey carrying jars. Original framing label (discarded) for N. Howley ph. 065 21172 Ennis Hardware Stores 14 Parnell Street Ennis Co. Clare Ireland. signature looks like Tawdu Bekele Ethiopia Copilot says E.C. means Ethiopian calendar which dates the piece to 1969/70. It says it is likely to be from the Addis Ababa School of Fine Arts, where many printing workshops etc were held at this time. Probably a student piece. Sheet 34x41cm (H x W).
