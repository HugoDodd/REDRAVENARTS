Untitled

Hubert Nii Abbey

1972

Mixed media on paper

46 × 41 cm

Dated 1972.

Purchased DN Bristol 1/15. £20.30 in mixed lot in dispersal of works from Denis Bowen’s estate. The lot included mostly Bowens but also a Scottie Wilson. Painted in the style of Zwei Frauen 1955 by Beuys (sold Christies Amsterdam April 2014).

Attributed: listed in the stock document as "Beuys, Joseph (attributed)". Inscription in German. Inscription reads ‘Wirkung des Vollmonds auf den Kopf des Schamanen Beuys’ i.e. ‘Impact of the full moon on the head of the shamen Beuys’. Christies said the writing looked to be that of Beuys (indeed it does) but would need verification by Galerie Thaddeus Roppac which runs the estate. This gallery in February 2020 said they could no longer offer verification due to the legal disputes that had arisen from such judgements.
