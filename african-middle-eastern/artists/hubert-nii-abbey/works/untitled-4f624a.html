Untitled

Hubert Nii Abbey

Woodblock

40 × 26 cm

Signed in print.

A British expat family who lived in Hong Kong c. 1960s.

Further censor seal, artist marks and characters outside of the main print. Dawson’s 6/21 had a print from the same series with identical markings in the margins and they described it as being published by Takeuchi. If so, this is Takeuchi Magohachi, essentially the original printer in 1833-34 (see British Museum info). MADDY
