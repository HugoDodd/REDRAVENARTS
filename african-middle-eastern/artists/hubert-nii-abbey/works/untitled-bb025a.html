Untitled

Hubert Nii Abbey

Oil on board

49 × 65 cm

Signed Castano.

Label verso for The Pantechnicon (a storage facility in Chelsea) gives owner as L Granville Grossman and date of 12.6.74. This suggests it was in the collection of property developer and art collector Leonard Granville Grossman. (His wife, herself a noted art collector, has a members’ room in the Tate Modern named after her).
