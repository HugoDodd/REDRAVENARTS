Hubert Nii Abbey

Ink and gold leaf on paper

20 × 15 cm

Ladies bathing, illustrated page from book. W. Gaze 7/23. google translate seems to suggest this is a page from a handwritten and illustrated Persian translation of Victor Hugo’s Les Miserables. The name Kolahi is at the bottom of one page, suggesting a possible author. The scene could represent a brothel.
