Untitled

Hubert Nii Abbey

Ink on paper

28 × 38 cm

Unsigned.

Illustrated in anthropologist Geoffrey Gorer’s 1936 book ‘Bali and Angkor: A 1930s Pleasure Trip Looking at Life and Death’. Here it is described as “A modern Balinese drawing in Chinese ink of a Kris dance”. In the book Gorer says about this work “the extraordinary technical ability is, I hope, evident even after double reproduction”.

Kris Dancers. Sticker marked Gorer on backboard. School of Ida Bagus Made Togug (possibly Ida Bagus Made Bala)
