Hubert Nii Abbey

Ink on paper

33 × 44 cm

Tregeare Estate, Launceston, Cornwall. Previous owner was a Hong Kong Business Man, according to the vendor.

Chinese text. Text says a portrait of Zheng Banqiao (Qing dynasty artist – 1700s), whose accomplishments it lauds. The chop says the calligrapher is Zi Kuan.
