Untitled

Hubert Nii Abbey

Watercolour

60 × 45 cm

One of four watercolours showing hunting and ceremonial scenes (one is signed NJIMONGNA, ISSA, NJIMBAM, FDAN). Appear to be scenes of King Njoya (1883-1931) and Bamoun country, Western uplands. Probably painted c.1980s/90s. Njimbam, Issa and Njimongna are all Cameroonian names. Copilot research says these are Foumban (Bamum) Palace scene traditions from the 1970s-1990s and that they are workshop pieces where a lead artist (in this case Nji Mongna) would take the lead and an assistant (probably Nji Mbam) might do pattern infill. Cameroon.
