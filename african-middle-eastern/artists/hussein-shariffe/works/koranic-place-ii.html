Koranic Place II

Hussein Shariffe

Oil on canvas

101 × 75 cm

Signed and titled verso.

Bought by Gidon Stemmer’s father Zalman from, he believed, Drian Gallery, London in the 1960s. In fact would have been Gallery One where it is in the 1963 solo show by Shariffe as a 1963 painting by him. + £50 refurb = £1300

Shariffe family live nr Kilburn, NHS staff etc. Seen by JHW but don’t have much stuff.
