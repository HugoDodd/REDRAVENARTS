Ignatius Sserulyo

1937 – 2018

Artist Biography

Igantius Sserulyo studied at the Margaret Trowell School of Fine Art at Makerere University in Kampala, Uganda from 1960-64 and was to be associated with the school throughout his career. From 1969-74 he took an MA in Fine Art there and he taught at the University from 1973-2002, including a stint as head from 1982-86. One of the finest Ugandan artists to emerge in the 1960s, Sserulyo was Visiting Artist at the Ruskin School of Drawing and Fine Art, Oxford in 1982 and also had a solo exhibition at Wolfson College, Oxford in that year. He became best known for large-scale murals and some sculpture, often showing contemporary social scenes in a semi-realist manner. His work has been exhibited relatively widely in mixed exhibitions, apparently including the Whitechapel Gallery, London, Malmo Kunsthall, Sweden and the Guggenheim Museum in New York.
