At the Car Park

Ignatius Sserulyo

1969

Oil on board

122 x 92 cm

Signed and dated ’69

Titled on label verso, listing the artist's address as Makerere University.

Provenance: Brought to Essex, England, from Uganda in the late 1970s by an expatriate English family who lived and worked in Kampala during the presidency of Idi Amin.
