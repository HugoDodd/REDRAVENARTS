At the car park

Ignatius Sserulyo

1969

Oil on board

122 × 92 cm

Signed, dated ’69. Titled on label verso, where artist address given as Makerere University.

Bought back to Essex from Uganda in the late 1970s by an ex-pat English family who lived and worked in Kampala under Idi Amin.

Uganda
