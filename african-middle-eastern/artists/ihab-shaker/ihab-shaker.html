Ihab Shaker

B. 1933

Artist Biography

Ihab Shaker was born in Cairo, Egypt and after secondary education attended the Italian-run Leonardo Da Vinci School of Art in the city, graduating in 1957. From 1954 he had been contributing political cartoons to the Al-Gomhouriya journal but in 1960 he switched to Sabah El-Kheir, a magazine he was to have a long association with. Shaker – who lived in Paris from 1969-1976 before returning to Cairo – went on to have a career as an illustrator, cartoonist, film animator and an artist. His work always had an exaggerated, plastic nature to it. Shaker had eight solo art exhibitions from the 1970s in Japan, Vienna, Paris, Spain and Jordan as well as in Egypt.
