Israel Ala (Albert Odunsi)

1918–c.1970s

Artist Biography

Albert Odunsi (later Israel Ala), one of Nigeria’s pioneer modern artists, was born in Lagos. He studied in the late 1930s and 1940s under Aina Onabolu, widely regarded as the founding father of modern Nigerian art. Onabolu cited one of Odunsi’s early paintings, Arrest, the Return, Accident, as a key example of the dynamic subject matter he was teaching to his students.

In 1948, Odunsi contributed to the first-ever group exhibition of Nigerian artists, organized by John A. Danford for the British Council in Lagos. His landscape painting Going to the Market was included in the exhibition catalogue under the name Israel Ala (also known as Albert Odunsi), marking the transition to his adopted name. Northwestern University in Illinois, USA, holds a 1947 drawing signed Albert Odunsi, likely among his last under that signature.

In 1963, Ala became a founding member of the Society of Nigerian Artists (SNA), formally inaugurated in January 1964 under the presidency of Yusuf Grillo. Ala’s work is held in the Federal Society for Art and Humanities (FSAH) collection at the University of Lagos Library—the earliest initiative to establish a public national collection of Nigerian art.
