Jacob Afolabi

B. 1940

Artist Biography

Jacob Afolabi was born in Ikirun, Nigeria and became the first artist to flourish at a workshop in Oshogbo in 1962, and hence a founder member of what was to become known as the Oshogbo School. Afolabi was a sculptor, painter and textile artist but gained greatest success with his linocuts and other printing techniques. He produced flowing images and morph-like figures, notable for their feeling of motion. Afolabi showed widely in mixed exhibitions in the US and Europe as well as in Nigeria. He was a key participant in the 2000 exhibition ‘A Concrete Vision: Oshogbo Art in the 1960s’ at the Smithsonian Museum in Washington. Afolabi’s work is held in the National gallery of Canada, Jean Pigozzi’s Contemporary African Art Collection, the Harmon Foundation and other major collections. His work was included in the major exhibition African Modernism in America 1947-67 held in 2023-24 at the Phillips Collection in Washington, United States and at Nigerian Modernism at the Tate Modern in London 2025-26.
