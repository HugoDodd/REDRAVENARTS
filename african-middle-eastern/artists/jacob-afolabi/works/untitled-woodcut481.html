Untitled

Jacob Afolabi

Woodcut

20 × 15 cm

Unsigned.

Adoration of the Magi? Numbered 18 verso. Attributed due to style and because bought in lot with three other Afolabis. Could this be “Unto Us A Child Is Born”, a woodcut exhibited at the March-April 1972 Exhibition and Sale of Contemporary Nigerian Art at the Africa Centre, Covent Garden, London. Clearly a scene of Mary, Joseph and Jesus and the three wise men.
