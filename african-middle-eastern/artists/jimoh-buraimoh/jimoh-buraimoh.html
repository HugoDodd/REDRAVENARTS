Jimoh Buraimoh

b.1943

Artist Biography

Chief Jimoh Buraimoh was born in Osogbo, Osun State, Nigeria and has become one of the most influential artists to emerge from the Osogbo School of Art. His work, primarily as a bead painter, intermingles Western media with Yoruba styles and motifs. He has exhibited widely, having solo shows in Nigeria, UK, Germany and USA, and has taken part in major mixed exhibitions – most notably ‘A Concrete Vision: Osogbo Art in the 1960s’ at the Smithsonian Museum in Washington in 2000. Buraimoh’s work is held in permanent collections at the Smithsonian, the National Museum in Lagos and National Gallery of Modern Art, Lagos. His work was also shown at Nigerian Modernism at the Tate Modern in London 2025-26.
