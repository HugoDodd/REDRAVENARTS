Jimoh Buraimoh

B. 1943

Artist Biography

Chief Jimoh Buraimoh was born in Osogbo, Osun State, Nigeria, and has become one of the most influential and celebrated masters to emerge from the Osogbo School of Art. Renowned globally as a pioneer of modern bead painting, his signature technique seamlessly fuses Western artistic media with traditional Yoruba glass beadworking traditions, iconography, and symbolic motifs.

Buraimoh has exhibited widely across international institutions, holding solo exhibitions in Nigeria, the United Kingdom, Germany, and the United States. He featured prominently in the landmark exhibition A Concrete Vision: Osogbo Art in the 1960s at the Smithsonian National Museum of African Art in Washington, D.C. (2000), and his work was presented in Nigerian Modernism at Tate Modern, London (2025–26).

His works are held in prestigious permanent collections worldwide, including the Smithsonian National Museum of African Art, the National Museum in Lagos, and the National Gallery of Modern Art, Lagos.
