Jorge Nhaca

1943–1997

Artist Biography

Jorge Nhaca was born in Lourenco Marques (now Maputo), the capital of what became Mozambique. Nhaca took part in the Nucleo D’Arte’s 1966 ‘First Modern Art Exhibition’ and, despite being relatively unknown, took the sculpture prize. Nhaca became a leading figure on the Maputo art scene and his work is held in Mozambique’s National Museum of Art. After his early death he was honoured with a special section in the 1999 Maputo biennale.
