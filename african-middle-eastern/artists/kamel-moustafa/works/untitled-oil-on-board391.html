Untitled

Kamel Moustafa

Oil on board

29 × 38 cm

Signed.

Collection of Dr Mohammed Said Farsi.

Portrait of a lady. Numbered 13 verso, price 600/g. Also Hamish Dewar, St James’ conservation label verso. it is likely this portrait is of Queen Farida (1921-1988) who married King Farouk in 1938. They were engaged in 1937 which seems a likely date for the painting given Mustafa was painting princess Fawzia Fuad around that time. (note: museum to Queen Farida and three books written by a friend, Dr Lotus Abdel Karim)
