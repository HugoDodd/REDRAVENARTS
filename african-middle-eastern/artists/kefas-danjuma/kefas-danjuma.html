Kefas Danjuma

1958 – 2016

Artist Biography

Kefas Nenpunmun Danjuma was born in Vom, Plateau State in Nigeria and from 1972-76 attended Saint John’s College in Jos. From there he went to Ahmadu Bello University (ABU) in Zaria, studying art and eventually gaining a PhD in art history. He went on to combine a teaching career at ABU with his painting and achieved success in both. Danjuma’s work was included in a Guiness Art Exhibition in Lagos in 1994 and in shows with three other ABU artists in 2010 and 2012 at the Thought Pyramid Art Gallery in Abuja. Danjuma considered humans the most important form that God created and his artwork concentrated on trying to capture their essence, manifesting itself often in studies of faces. A painting of his was included in a showcase exhibition of fellow ABU academic ‘Gani Odutokun and his influence’ that was shown at the Brunei Gallery, School of Oriental and African Studies in London in 1999 and the following year travelled to Brussels and then the Caribbean. Another work was shown at an exhibition ‘Showing Nigeria’ in the UK in 2012 to coincide with the London Olympics. His work was promoted by the top commercial gallery in Lagos, Signature Gallery, and he was both a member of the Nogh-Nogh art group in Zaria and a fellow of the Society of Nigerian Artists
