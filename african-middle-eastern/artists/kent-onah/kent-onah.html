Kent Onah

B. 1964

Artist Biography

Kent Ikenna Onah was born in Sept 1964. He is a lecturer at the School of Art & Design in Auchi, Edo State, Nigeria. Onah has had two solo exhibitions and 14 mixed exhibitions; one of the latter was at Worcester State College, USA in Spring 2005. Onah is a member of the Society of Nigerian Artists (SNA) and a leading light in the Pan African Circle of Artists (PACA, an organisation that brings together artists across Africa). He is known for his use of vibrant colours and is a leading member of the “Auchi school” of Nigerian artists.
