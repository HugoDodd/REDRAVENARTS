The Impalas

Louis Mwaniki

Metal sculpture

38 × 23 cm

Presented on an ebonised square plinth bearing incised signature Mwaniki, note on base gives title.

McTears were selling as “accompanied by purchase receipt from Kumbu Kumbu, Nairobi, dated 25/9/72” but this has been lost during delivery. A note attached to the base – presumably by a Kumba Kumba staff member – reads as follows: “‘THE IMPALAS’ by MWANIKI. This metal sculpture has been done by one among the African leading artists in the metal work. Mwaniki is the best as far as metal work is concerned. He comes from Kikuyu Tribe”.

Attributed: listed in the stock document as "Attrib. to Louis Mwaniki". 38cm wide x 23cm tall (27cm including base). There is one other Mwaniki, John Mwaniki, who was a sculptor who taught art at Starehe School for Boys in Nairobi. John Mwaniki was the first born son (c. 1933) of influential nationalist journalist Henry Muoria with his first wife Elizabeth Thogori. So, an outside chance this could be his work.
