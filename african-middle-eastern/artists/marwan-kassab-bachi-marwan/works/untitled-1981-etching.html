Marwan Kassab-Bachi (Marwan)

1981

Etching

19x26cm

signed and dated ’81

Provenance: collection of Syrian expat living in the West Country, sold by the widow. Looks to be part of the Marionette series. Adonis probably refers to the noted Syrian poet Adonis, with whom Marwan had close links and correspondence, and perhaps was originally given to him.
