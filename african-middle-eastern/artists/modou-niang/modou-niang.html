Modou Niang

B. 1945

Artist Biography

Modou Niang – full name Mamdou Cheikh Niang - is a Senegalese artist who is mostly known for his tapestries, often involving birds. He was one of the first African students at the School of Arts in Dakar, participated in the first World Festival of Black Arts in 1966 and was among the pioneers of the Senegalese Decorative Arts centre at Thies in 1966. He now works from a studio in the Village of Arts in Dakar. Major exhibitions that Niang took part in include the Seventh Paris Biennale in 1971 and Galeries Nationales du Grand Palais, Paris 1974. Senegal in 1978 donated one of his large tapestries to UNESCO.
