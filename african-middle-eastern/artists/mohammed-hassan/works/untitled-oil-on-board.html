Untitled

Mohammed Hassan

Oil on board

68 × 43 cm

Signed Hassan in Roman and Mohammed Hassan in Arabic.

Collection of Dr Mohammed Said Farsi. Abdo (1919-99) was a famous Egyptian singer and actress.

The sitter is Raja’ (sometimes spelt Ragaa) Abdo. Numbered 43 verso, also Arabic writing saying ‘from Madam/Yusuf Bek’ and price Gg 1,500. Another hand-written inscription says Mohammed Hassan 1 in Arabic. Hamish Dewar, St James’ conservation label verso. Part of consignment label remains, which gives artist details and says it is a ‘Portr .... Abdo’. a 19x28cm 1926 portrait by Hassan, also from the Farsi collection, was sold at Mallams 22nd October 2025, Lot 189, for £4.400 hammer)
