Palm March

Olayinka Burney-Nicol

1984

Monoprint

43x61cm

signed, dated ’84

Provenance: Wimbledon Village collector. Purchased Wimbledon Auctions 8/24.

Exhibition: March 1984 Society of Sierra Leone Artists exhibition held at the British Council Hall in Freetown; April-Nov 1984 touring exhibition Sierra Leonean Artists in Britain which showed at the Commonwealth Institute in London and then at venues in Leeds, Durham and Hull.
