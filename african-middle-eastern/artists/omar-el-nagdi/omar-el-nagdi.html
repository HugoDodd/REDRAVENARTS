Omar el-Nagdi

1931–2019

Artist Biography

Omar el-Nagdi graduated from the Faculty of Fine Arts in Cairo in 1953 and went on to study in Russia and Italy. A member of Cairo’s Liberal Artist’s group, el-Nagdi is a multi-disciplinary artist, musician and philosopher whose most famous works emerged from calligraphy in the 1960s and 1970s. A prize winner at the Alexandria Biennale in 1966, 1968 and 1975, el-Nagdi’s work has been exhibited widely internationally and he is seen as one of the most influential of modern day Egyptian artists. Recent solo shows include those in 2009 and 2010 at the Al Masar Gallery in Cairo. El-Nagdi’s works are held by the Museum of Modern Art in Cairo, Venice and South Korea, the Museum of Fine Art in Alexandria and public institutions in Italy, USA and the UK. His work is also in the Barjeel Collection and was displayed at Christies London in 2023 as part of the ‘Modern and Contemporary Art in the Arab World’ exhibition organised by the Barjeel Art Foundation.
