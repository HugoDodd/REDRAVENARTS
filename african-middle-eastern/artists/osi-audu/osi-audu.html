Osi Audu

B. 1956

Artist Biography

Osi Audu was born in Nigeria in 1956. He studied fine art at the University of Ife (now Obafemi Awolowo University) in Ile-Ife, Nigeria, before earning his Master of Fine Arts degree at the University of Georgia in the United States. Now based in New York, Audu describes his work as compositions impacted by the abstract geometric possibilities inherent in African art and cultural objects, exploring the concept of the head as the primary center of waking and dreaming consciousness.

Audu’s work has been presented in prominent international exhibitions, including the Gwangju Biennale in South Korea (1995) and the 56th Venice Biennale (2015). He has participated in numerous major group exhibitions across the United States, the United Kingdom, Japan, Germany, and internationally.

His works are represented in major public and institutional collections worldwide, including the Smithsonian National Museum of African Art in Washington, D.C., the British Museum in London, the Nigerian High Commission in London, and the National Gallery of Art in Lagos.
