Paul Igboanugo

B. 1940

Artist Biography

Paul Igboanugo was born in Delta State and raised in Oraifite, Anambra State, Nigeria. He earned a diploma in art from the Yaba College of Technology in 1965 and graduated in 1972 with a Fine Art degree from the Nsukka School at the University of Nigeria. In 1979–1980, he spent a year studying printmaking at Croydon College in the UK on a British Council grant. A prominent member of the Society of Nigerian Artists and a longtime university educator in Nigeria, Igboanugo became one of the foremost proponents of modern Uli art.

Describing his body of work as 'a celebration of life', Igboanugo was particularly renowned for his printmaking techniques, including woodcuts and etchings. His work was incorporated into the National Collection upon the founding of the National Gallery of Modern Art in Lagos in 1981. A major solo exhibition of his prints and drawings, Drums of Life, was held at the National Museum in Lagos in 1992.

Igboanugo exhibited extensively in Nigeria and internationally, participating in touring exhibitions to Romania and Cuba (1976), Morley Gallery, London (1980), Bonn, Germany (1982), Birmingham, Alabama (1984), Hanover Gallery, London (1990), and the Commonwealth Institute, London (1991). His works are held in public and institutional collections, including the National Gallery of Modern Art in Lagos, as well as collections in Bayreuth and Frankfurt, Germany.
