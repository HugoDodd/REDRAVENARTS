Paul Igboanugo

b.1940

Artist Biography

Paul Igboanugo was born in Delta State but raised in Oraifite in Anambra State, Nigeria. He took a diploma in art at Yaba College of Technology in 1965, then graduated in 1972 with a degree in fine art from Nsukka School, University of Nigeria. In 1979-80 he spent a year on a British Council grant studying printmaking at Croydon College, UK. A member of the Society of Nigerian Artists, Igboanugo had a long career teaching in Nigerian universities and became one of the foremost proponents of modern Uli art. His work, which he described as “a celebration of life”, was included in the National Collection on the inception of the National Gallery of Modern Art in Lagos in 1981. Igboanugo exhibited widely within Nigeria. Elsewhere, his work was shown in mixed exhibitions included a touring exhibition to Romania and Cuba in 1976, Morley Gallery, London 1980, Bonn, Germany 1982, Birmingham, Alabama, USA in 1984, Hanover Gallery, London in 1990 and the Commonwealth Institute, London in 1991. A major solo exhibition of his prints and drawings entitled ‘Drums of Life’ was held at the National Museum in Lagos in 1992. Igboanugo was best known for his printing techniques, notably etchings and woodcuts. His work is held in various public collections in Nigeria, including the National Gallery in Lagos, and also in Bayreuth and Frankfurt in Germany.
