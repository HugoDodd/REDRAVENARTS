Rana Chalabi

B. 1958

Artist Biography

Rana Chalabi is a Syrian/Lebanese artist who graduated in architecture and art from the American University in Beirut in 1981, but whose primary artistic residence and presence has been in Cairo. Chalabi’s painting and sculpture is dominated by her interest in movement, both physical and spiritual, and a fascination with the circle as a symbol of perfection. A devotee of Sufism, dervishes and dancers make up the bulk of her art though the movement often renders the images largely abstract. Chalabi has had numerous solo exhibitions in Cairo and elsewhere in Egypt and international exhibitions include the UK, France, Austria, Japan, Lebanon, Jordan and the Arab-American Museum in the United States.
