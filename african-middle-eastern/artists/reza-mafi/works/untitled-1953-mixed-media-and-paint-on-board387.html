Untitled

Reza Mafi

1953

Mixed media and paint on board

48 × 22 cm

Signed in Farsi and dated 53 (1974).

Private family collection since 1970s. Previously unsold at Rosebery’s 4/22 at estimate £7-9k.

Calligraphic abstract. Mounted on card backboard covered in hessian. the date ’53 is a shortening of 1353 in the Persian calendar, which translates to 1974 in the west.
