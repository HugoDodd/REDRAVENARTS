Roger Botembe

1959–2019

Artist Biography

Roger Botembe was born in Leopoldsville (now Kinshasa) in what is now the Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC). Initially self-taught, from 1981-82 he studied in the workshop of Samir Zarouur at the National Institute of Arts in Abidjan, Cote d’Ivoire and later was awarded a diploma after study at the Royal Academy of Fine Arts in Brussels, Belgium. Botembe returned to the DRC and began teaching at the Academy of Fine Arts in Kinshasa. Whilst lecturing there, in 1992 he also set up his own modern art school known as the Ateliers Botembe where he created and taught a style known as “trans symbolism”. In essence, this was the illustration of characters in the form of masks. From 2006 Botembe was also a director of the National Museums of Congo. He achieved early success, first exhibiting in 1978, and went on to contribute to a string of mixed exhibitions including in Belgium, France, Monaco, Haiti, Cote d’Ivoire, South Africa, Congo and the UK. As one of the DRC’s most important artists, he won numerous medals and awards, notably from the Belgian government, UNESCO and at the1995 Brazzaville Biennale. Major solo shows included the National Museum in Kinshasa in 1999, MOMO Gallery in Johannesburg, South Africa in 2007 and Galerie Arts Pluriels in Abidjan, Cote d’Ivoire in 2010. MOMO Gallery held a major retrospective in 2021-2022 following his death.
