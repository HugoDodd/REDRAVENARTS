Tahreer

Saadi Al-Kaabi

1974

Oil on canvas

68 × 95 cm

Signed in English and Arabic, dated ‘74. Titled in Arabic verso, where also written raqm ithnayn (number two) and marked 17.

From a very good private collection according to the auctioneer.

Abstract. Tahreer means liberation, often used in a nationalist anti-colonial sense. Tahreer Square Cairo etc. (needs reframing after Adrian botch) (a 65x80cm work sold 6/21 at Bonhams £18k hammer)
