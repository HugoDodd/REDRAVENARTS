Salem Salah

1948–2013

Artist Biography

Salem Salah, full name Salem Hassan Abdel-Rahman Salah, grew up in Gharbia in northern Egypt but moved to London in 1970. He stayed in London until returning to Egypt in 1996. Whilst in London he studied at Chelsea College of Art from 1973-77 and then in 1980 was awarded a Masters at the Central School of Art and Design in London. Salah combined being an artist with art criticism and lecturing and later became a museum director in Cairo. Solo shows included the African Cultural Centre in London in 1984 and Khan el-Maghraby in Cairo in 1998, plus two in London’s Notting Hill in 1992 and 2007. He had numerous mixed exhibitions in Cairo and Alexandria but also participated in shows in Canada, Paris and London, including the Royal Academy Summer Exhibition in 1977, 1978 and 1986. Wagdy Soliman included a piece by Salah in his 2019 exhibition ‘Egyptian Inspiration’ at the Egyptian Cultural Centre in London. His work is also in public collections in the UK, Spain and Egypt’s Modern Art Museum in Cairo.
