The Brook

Sam Ntiro

1956

Oil on canvas

61 x 51 cm

Signed twice

Titled verso on period exhibition label: "The origin of this spring is perhaps at the top of Mt. Kilimanjaro. It flows through thick, rich vegetation (Kilimanjaro 1956)".

Exhibition and gallery label details: numbered 54; frame inscribed "Deitch 2538B". Originally exhibited at Piccadilly Gallery, London.
