Nude

Samir Ali (Samir Ali Nuobi)

1974

Black crayon

46 × 43 cm

Signed, titled and dated 1974.

Arabic writing verso titles the painting ’black peasant’ and gives the artist name as Ali. identification is by Google AI - artist Samir Ali Noubi (or Nubi), usually known as Samir Ali. Pretty obscure but see Duroub Gallery and Al Marsam Gallery in Cairo.
