Samir Nanoo

1944–2010

Artist Biography

Samir Nanoo was born in Baghdad, Iraq. He studied microbiology in Belgium and worked as a scientist and painted in his spare time. He moved to Hessen, Germany in 1980 and became a full-time artist. Although he never returned to live in Iraq permanently, Nanoo always considered himself an Iraqi artist and had a studio in Tunisia as well as in Germany. His exhibiting career was mainly in Germany and Austria, although he also had a solo show in Turin in 1995 and later mixed shows in Tunisia. His works used vibrant colours and were painted in a loose, expressionistic style.
