Suad Al-Attar

B. 1942

Artist Biography

Suad Al-Attar was born in Iraq and studied at both Baghdad and California State Universities. She was in 1965 the first Iraqi woman to ever have a solo exhibition but in 1976 left with her family and relocated to London. Her many prizes include first prize at the international Biennale at Cairo in 1984 and she has exhibited extensively internationally. Much of Suad’s painting is characterised by an intense dreamlike and poetic sensibility that draws on motifs and symbols from within the traditions of Middle Eastern art.
