Theresa Musoke

B. 1942

Artist Biography

Theresa Musoke was born in Kampala and was one of only a few women to study art at Makerere University’s Department of Fine Art in the early 1960s. Her talent was quickly noted and she was commissioned in 1964 to paint a birth mural for Makerere University’s Mary Stuart Hall. She later created works for the National Parks of Kenya and Entebbe International Airport in Uganda. Musoke was given a solo show in 1965 at the prestigious Paa ya Paa (PAP) Art Centre in Nairobi, becoming the first woman to have a solo show in independent Kenya. She then spent a year in the UK on a Commonwealth scholarship gaining a diploma in printmaking from the Royal College of Art, London and later studied also at the University of Pennsylvania in the USA.
