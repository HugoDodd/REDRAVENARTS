Twins Seven Seven

1944 – 2011

Artist Biography

Twins Seven Seven was born in Ijara, Nigeria and was to become one of Nigeria’s foremost artists and musicians. He learned his art attending the 1964 Mbari-Mbayo workshop under the tutelage of Georgina Beier and rapidly became one of the best known of the Oshogbo school artists. He stood out amongst his contemporaries for his individual style and fervid imagination. His works across a wide variety of media were rooted in Yoruba religion and folklore but Twins claimed to be the first modern artist in Africa; more realistically, he called his work “contemporary Yoruba traditional art”. He was made a UNESCO Ambassador for his art in 2006. Major exhibitions were held at the Museum of Modern Art in New York, the Pompidou Centre in Paris, the National Modern Art Gallery in Lagos, the Institute of Contemporary Art in London and at prestigious venues in Japan and Australia. A work by Twins Seven Seven was shown at the Venice Biennale in 2024. His work was also shown at Nigerian Modernism at the Tate Modern in London 2025-26. Permanent collections holding Twins Seven Seven’s work include the Brooklyn Museum in New York, the Philadelphia Museum of Art, the Museum of Fine Arts Saint Petersburg, Florida and the Smithsonian Institute in the United States, the Jean Pigozzi collection and the University of London in the UK.
