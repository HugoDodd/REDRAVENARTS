The Anti Bird Ghost

Twins Seven Seven

Etching

30 × 38 cm

Signed and titled recto. Numbered 100/17.

Printed on paper stamped Graphikreis der Neuer Munchner Galerie. Almost certainly produced for the Neuer Munchner Galerie exhibition in 1965 ‘Moderne Kunst aus Oshogbo’, the first major exhibition abroad that Twins Seven Seven took part in.
