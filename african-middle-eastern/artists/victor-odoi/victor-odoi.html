Victor Odoi

B. 1965

Artist Biography

Victor Odoi was born in Ghana and lives in Accra. His expressionistic paintings depict scenes inspired by everyday life, whether of women in a market, fishermen at work or similar. Odoi studied art at Ghanatta College of Arts, his teachers including Amon Kotei and Krotel Ketteh. He is one of Ghana’s better known artists and his work has been shown in mixed exhibitions internationally, including in the UK, Belgium, Australia and the United States. He also had a solo show in the International Airport in Accra.
