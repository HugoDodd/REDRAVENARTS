Wajih Nahle

1932–2017

Artist Biography

Wajih Nahle was born in Beirut, Lebanon and studied painting under the tutelage of Lebanese artist Moustafa Farroukh. Initially painting in a Western style, Nahle began to find his artistic voice in painting decorative Islamic bas-reliefs and later creating Arabic calligraphy in an expressionist manner. In later years, and as his success grew, Nahle moved on to bolder and larger expressionist works. He had over 60 solo shows in the Arab world and beyond, one in Paris in 1977 selling out on the first day, and won numerous prestigious prizes. His work is held by the Grand Palais, Paris, Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York, Museum of Contemporary Art, Tunisia and numerous public collections in the Gulf.
