Latifa

Wajih Nahle

Oil/mixed media on canvas laid on board

82 × 120 cm

Signed. Verso marked I, titled?.

Calligraphic design. ‘Latifa’ and marked 1950/67. + restoration = £60 = £762 This has a Quranic Sufic inscription from the surah al-Falaq (dawn). Perhaps the 50/67 has some link to the Wally Findlay Gallery as in 1977 it had an exhibition of Nahle’s work titled The Islamic Work of Wajih Nahle 1952/77 and the next year 1953/78. All three of the calligraphic works are likely to be from c.1965-1975
