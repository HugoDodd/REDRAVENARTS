Ya Mujeeb

Wasma’a Khalid Chorbachi

2019

Oil on board laid on painted canvas panel

60 × 50 cm

Signed.dated 19V7 (1976 in Arabic).

Islamic pattern. Title on label verso translates as “O, God” (Mujeeb being another word for the Almighty). Vendor bought at Chorbachi’s solo exhibition in Abu Dhabi in 1976. Iraq/Egypt/USA
