Oba of Benin

Yinka Adeyemi

Linocut

41 x 51 cm (image) / 45 x 56 cm (sheet)

Signed and titled

Depicts the Oba, the traditional ruler of the Edo people in Nigeria. A copy of this print is held in the permanent collection of the Smithsonian National Museum of African Art, Washington, D.C.
