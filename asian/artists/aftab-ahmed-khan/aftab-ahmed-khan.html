Aftab Ahmed Khan

1935–2019

Artist Biography

Aftab Ahmed Khan (A A Khan) was born in Peshawar, India (now Pakistan) and was a member of the Pakistan Art Society from the late 1950s. He was initially drawn to photography and it was for his achievements in this field that he later received a Presidential ‘Pride of Performance’ award. However, Khan proved to be something of a polymath – he spent thirty years working as a forensic expert with the Pakistani police whilst writing, doing photography, painting and then in later years becoming a renowned calligraphic expert.

Clifton Art Gallery in Karachi in 1999 showed A. A. Khan’s paintings in a mixed exhibition called ‘Senior Artists’. In 2015 Khan had a solo exhibition at the Rawalpindi Arts Council showing photographs, paintings and calligraphy. This was followed in 2019 by a solo exhibition in Islamabad at the Pakistan National Museum of Ethnology (previously the Folk Art Museum). In 2023 he was given a major posthumous retrospective at the National Art Gallery in Islamabad where his works were shown alongside sculptures by his son Ahsan Khan.
