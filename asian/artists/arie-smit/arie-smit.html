Arie Smit

1916 – 2016

Artist Biography

Arie Smit was born a Dutch national but settled in Indonesia in the 1940s, taking out citizenship in 1950. He had trained in Europe and subsequently kept in touch with leading artistic trends but gradually took on more characteristics of the local painters. His work concentrated on Indonesian figures and landscapes and a permanent room in the art gallery in Denpasar, Bali is dedicated to his work.
