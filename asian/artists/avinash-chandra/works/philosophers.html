Philosophers

Avinash Chandra

1962

Pen, ink & watercolour

33 × 59 cm

Signed, dated ’62. Signed again and dated Jan ’62 verso.

Almost certainly from Chandra’s 1962 solo show at Molton Gallery. Very similar to another ’62 piece of same size called Philosophers that went unsold at Sotheby’s NBS £4-6k 2/5/08.

Label for Molton Galleries on backboard with title, drawing 4/10 and price of 25 guineas.
