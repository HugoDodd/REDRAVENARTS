Untitled

Avinash Chandra

1963

Pen, ink & watercolour

106 × 72 cm

Signed, dated ’63.

A gift from Chandra to the grandfather of the 2011 vendor. Possibly might have been an unsold from Hamilton Galleries’ ’63 show.

Nudes. on two sheets of joined paper. (hammer £1400 - estimated £2-3k, having failed at Sothebys London 31st May 2011 at £5-7k) Was originally to have come with a photograph and catalogue but vendor decided he wanted to retain.
