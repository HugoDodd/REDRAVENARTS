Burigude Zhang

b. 1961

Artist Biography

Burigude Zhang was born in Inner Mongolia, China in 1961. He worked initially as a high school art teacher before in 1982 being accepted at the prestigious Central Academy of Fine Arts in Beijing. After graduation he taught at the Beijing Union University then in 1994 took a scholarship which lead to an MA at Bradford College of Art in England in 1998. He worked there as an artist in residence until emigrating to Canada in 2001. Now based in Toronto his work marries traditional Chinese techniques with Western abstraction, often to beautiful effect. He had four solo shows in the UK, one in Canada in 2009 and another at the Duolun Museum of Modern Art in Shanghai in 2009. Numerous mixed shows in recent years in China include Contemporary Art Museum, Beijing in 2011 and others in Beijing and Jiangsu in 2012 and 2013. His work is in permanent public collections in the UK, Canada, Czech Republic and a number of institutions in China including the National Art Museum and the Museum of Contemporary Art (MOCA) in Bejing.
