Chen Kuan

B. 1959

Artist Biography

Chen Kuan is a Guilin-based artist and educator who taught at the Central Technology and Art College (possibly the Guangxi Arts College in Nanning). Documentation notes him as a Member of the Guangxi Art Association of China, Permanent Committeeman on the World Art & Culture Exchange Association, and Director of the Guilin Youth Art Association. Some sources additionally credit him as the Director of the Chinese Painters Association and Executive Vice President of the Bagui Painting Academy.

In 1997, his work was featured in the exhibition ‘Masterpieces of 20th Century Chinese Painting’, organized by the Canadian Foundation for the Preservation of Chinese Art and Historical Treasures, which toured in both Toronto and Hong Kong. Works by Chen Kuan are held in institutional public collections, including the Berkeley Art Museum in California and the Canadian Museum of Civilization in Quebec.
