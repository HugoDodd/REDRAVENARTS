Chen Kuan

b.1959

Artist Biography

A Guilin-based teacher at the Central Technology and Art College (possibly the Guangzi Arts College in Nanning?), one source claims he was the Director of the Chinese Painters Association and Executive VP of the Bagui Painting Academy. The label on my painting verso has Chen as being a Member of the Guangxi Art Association of China, Permanent Committeeman on the World Art & Culture Exchange Association and Director of Guilin Youth Art Association. Also that he had “great big entry in the dictionary of Chinese artists”. An exhibition titled ‘Masterpieces of 20th century Chinese Painting’, was organised in 1997 by the Canadian Foundation for the Preservation of Chinese Art and Historical Treasures in Toronto – it had a Chen Kuan picture in it and was shown in both Canada and Hong Kong. Works by Chen Kuan are held in the Berkeley Art Museum, California and the Canadian Museum of Civilisation, Quebec.
