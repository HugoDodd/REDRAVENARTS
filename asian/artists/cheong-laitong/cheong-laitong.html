Cheong Laitong

b.1932

Artist Biography

Cheong Laitong was born in Guangzhou, China in 1932 and moved with the family to Kuala Lumpur, Malaya in 1938. Cheong joined the Wednesday Art Group under Peter Harris, one of the earliest and most influential artistic societies in the country. He achieved renown when in 1957 he was chosen in open competition to create a large mural to adorn the front facade of the newly opened National Museum. A scholarship in 1961 enabled Cheong to study at the Central Art School in London and he took first prize in oil and acrylic painting at the Salon Malaysia in both 1969 and 1979. Although he earned a living as an art director for Rothmans, Cheong continued to paint. He has had, and continues to have, numerous solo shows in Kuala Lumpur, the earliest being in 1966 and most recent in 2014.
