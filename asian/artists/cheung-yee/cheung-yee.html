Cheung Yee

b. 1936

Artist Biography

Cheung Yee was born in 1936 in Guangzhou and graduated in 1958 from the Fine Art of Taiwan Normal University. He moved to work in Hong Kong in the early 1960s, his sculptures marrying Western modern art with traditional Chinese forms. A growing international reputation led to solo exhibitions in USA, London, Singapore, Manila and Taiwan as well as Hong Kong, plus participation in numerous group shows. He continues to work, both on paper and in sculpture, and is recognised as having been one of the leading lights of Hong Kong and Chinese modernism.
