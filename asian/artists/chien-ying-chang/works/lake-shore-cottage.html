Lake shore cottage

Chien-Ying Chang

Watercolour

63 × 63 cm

Seller bought at the Leicester Galleries solo exhibition of October 1960, where it is listed as No. 3. Catalogue included in sale.

2x chops and Chinese characters. Leicester Galleries, London label verso gives artist details, title and purchaser W. F. Newbury. Lake shore cottage appears to be the same view as Silent stream.
