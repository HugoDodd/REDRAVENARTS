Chihung Yang

b. 1947

Artist Biography

Chihung Yang is a Taiwanese-born artist who studied at the National Taiwan College of Art 1965-68, but has been based in New York since 1979. A highly regarded painter, he works in a bold expressionist style, often on a large scale. Yang has had solo exhibitions across the US, in Taiwan, Hong Kong, Korea, Canada, UK and elsewhere. Recent high profile one-man shows include National Art Museum of China, Beijing in 2007, China Square Gallery, New York in 2008 and a forthcoming 2011 exhibition at the National Taiwan Museum of Fine Arts, Taichung. His work is held in public collections in Taiwan, the US and Singapore.
