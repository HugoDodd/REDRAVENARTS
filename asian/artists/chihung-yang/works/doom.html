Doom

Chihung Yang

Charcoal, pastels, acrylic on paper

96 × 126 cm

Signed, dated 1984-85.

Probably purchased at Galleri Mustad’s 1986 Summer Group Show, at which Yang exhibited. Failed to sell at Sweden’s Stockholm Auksionsverk in 2004, supposedly sold at Sothebys in July 2005 for £800 (at £1-2k estimate).

Media, title, artist, dates etc all on Galleri Mustad, Molnlycke, Sweden label verso.
