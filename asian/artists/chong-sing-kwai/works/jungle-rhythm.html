Jungle Rhythm

Chong Sing Kwai

1960

Oil on board

87 × 66 cm

Signed, dated ’60.

Label verso gives title, artist name, address and cost $225. Address at Ministry of Information, Brockman Road, K.L.(since changed to Jalan Dato’ Onn) suggests he might have been a government official. Malay $225 would then have equated to just over £26.
