Dil Bahadur Chitrakar

1926–2010

Artist Biography

D.B. Chitrakar was born in Patan, Nepal and was one of the first Nepali artists to move away from the Chitrakar tradition of religious paintings towards a more Western and modern style. He studied as a teenager under Tej Bahadur Chitrakar and then joined the Juddha Kala Pathsala (now known as the Lalit Kala Campus) art school in Kathmandu, graduating in 1950. He had a first solo exhibition at the prestigious Max Gallery in Durbar Merg in 1964, showing landscapes of Nepal, but a 1965 visit to Europe on a UNESCO grant opened his eyes to Western art. He began experimenting in abstracts and won the Nepal Association of Fine Art’s National Art Competition in 1966.

He was chosen as one of two artists to decorate King Birendra's Narayanhiti Palace and, although he also taught and worked as an illustrator, he continued to paint throughout his life. His final solo show was in 2007.
