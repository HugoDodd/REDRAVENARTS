Edgardo Sarmiento

b.1940

Artist Biography

Eddie Sarmiento was born in Malabon in May 1940 and graduated in fine arts at the University of Santo Tomas in 1961. He subsequently opened a gallery in Mabini in the 1960s. His art developed along classic Mabini lines and Sarmiento sold paintings or was commissioned to paint murals for varied clients including Manila Bank and the United States’ Clark Air Base. Sarmiento is currently represented by the Art Circle Gallery in Manila. Eddie’s on Alwynder is also a well-known artist.
