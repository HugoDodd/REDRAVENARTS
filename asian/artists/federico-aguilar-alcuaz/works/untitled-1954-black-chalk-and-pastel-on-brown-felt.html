Federico Aguilar Alcuaz

1954

Black chalk and pastel on brown felt

61 × 45 cm

Signed, dated ’54.

Portrait of a boy. Label for Wah Cheong no 50 Wellington Street Hong Kong attached on the reverse of the frame. Aguilar won 1st prize Roadside Squatters at 4th SNSAC Modern Painting Category in 1954. A 25x30cm similar ’54 piece sold Manila 9/15. k hammer
