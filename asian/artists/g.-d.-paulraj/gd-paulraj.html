G. D. Paulraj

1914–1989

Artist Biography

G. D. Paulraj was born to an artistic family in Kodaikanal, a hill station in the state of Tamil Nadu, India. He studied at the Chennai (then called Madras) Government School of Arts under Debi Prasad Roy Chowdhury. Although an artist in oils as well as watercolour, it is for the latter he is best known. Paulraj received much recognition in his lifetime, including a Mysore State Award, and he painted scenes for the British Queen Elizabeth and for a visiting Pope. His style was a mixture of realism and impressionism, and Paulraj was notable for his superb handling of light. His work can be found at the Victoria Technical Institute of Art in Chennai, in addition to the collections of the Papacy and the British Royal family.
