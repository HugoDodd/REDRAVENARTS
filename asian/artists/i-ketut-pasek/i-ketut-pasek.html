I Ketut Pasek

b.1966

Artist Biography

Ketut Pasek was born into a family of artisans in Mawang, a village near Ubud, the ancient Balinese Royal capital. After finishing school Pasek studied art at Batubulan Fine Art School. He also took lessons from senior Balinese painter Pande Andreja in Taman, near Ubud. His work was included in a major exhibition in 1991 at Batubulan Fine Art School, celebrating a notable anniversary. Pasek’s work is sold in a number of Japanese galleries and he had a solo exhibition in October 2009 at Ubud’s Komaneka Fine Art Gallery. His works have also been sold in major auction houses including Christies in Amsterdam and Borobudur Auctions in Singapore.
