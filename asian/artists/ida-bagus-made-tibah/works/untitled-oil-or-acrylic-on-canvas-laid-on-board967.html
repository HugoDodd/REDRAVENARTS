Untitled

Ida Bagus Made Tibah

Oil or acrylic on canvas laid on board

86 × 57 cm

Signed IDA BAGUS TIBAH, BATUAN, BALI.

Ceremonial dancers. Stamp on frame for HILTON HORDERN PTY Ltd, 92 Mount St, North Sydney, Australia. The painting looks c.1970s. A 1977 reference to Hilton Hordern being at 92 Mount St is the only current google reference to that address. The business for many years has been in Neutral Bay, Sydney. Hilton Hordern was a gallery and printing business set up by Marsden Hordern in 1958.
