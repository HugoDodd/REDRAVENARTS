J. Sultan Ali

1920–1990

Artist Biography

J. Sultan Ali was born in Mumbai, of Gujarati parents. He took a Diploma in Painting at the Government College of Arts and Crafts in Chennai (then Madras) 1939-45 and in 1954 became a member of the Progressive Painters’ Association there. He toured West Germany in 1968 on a grant from the Bonn government and also had a later British Council grant to visit the UK. Sultan Ali joined K.C.S. Panniker at the Cholmandal Artists’ Village, Madras in 1969. He exhibited widely internationally at group exhibitions in the UK, Germany and the USSR amongst others. He had numerous solo shows in India, and others in Los Angeles (1973) and Bayreuth, Germany (1989). In the last decade Delhi Art Gallery has held a regular series of posthumous shows of his work.
