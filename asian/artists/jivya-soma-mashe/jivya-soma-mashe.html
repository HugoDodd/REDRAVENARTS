Jivya Soma Mashe

1934–2018

Artist Biography

Jivya Soma Mashe was an artist from Maharashtra State in India who was the first to popularise the Warli tribal art form. Mashe painted from an early age, taking themes from tribal life and Warli legends but brought an unusual degree of sensitivity and movement to his art. His first major solo exhibition was at Gallery Chemould, Jehangir Art Gallery in Mumbai in 1975. This was followed by a show at the Palais de Menton in France in 1976 and subsequent exhibitions in Germany, Italy and the United States. His work was also part of the seminal Magiciens de la Terre at the Centre Pompidou in Paris in 1989. A final major show was held in 2007 at the Gallery Chemould, Mumbai. A major book on Mashe is due to be published in September 2025.
