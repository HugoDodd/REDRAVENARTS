Josaku Maeda

1926 – 2007

Artist Biography

Josaku Maeda was born in Toyama Prefecture and graduated from Musashino Art School in 1953. In 1957 he won a major prize at the First International Exhibition of young artists. He then turned to abstract painting on the theme of the mandala shape. A scholarship took him to France in 1958, and he was based in Paris for a number of years before returning to teach in Japan. A highly regarded academic and artist, Maeda won many prizes and exhibited widely until his death in 2007.
