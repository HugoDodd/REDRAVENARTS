Kam Kee Yong

b.1938

Artist Biography

Kam Kee Yong was born in Penang, Malaysia to a father who was a jazz clarinettist. Kam took quickly to the violin and in 1960 he studied the instrument at the Royal College of Music in London. At the same time he pursued a strong interest in art which, with music, he saw as two sides of the same coin. His first solo exhibition was in 1959 at the Penang Arts Council and whilst in London Kam had one man shows at the Woodstock Gallery in 1963, the Cathay Gallery in 1963 and 64 and Pugh and Carr Gallery in 1965. After moving to Singapore and as his musical career blossomed Kam continued to paint. He has subsequently had a number of solo shows in Singapore, including in 1972 at the Singaporean Art Society and in 1994 at the Sweegan Gallery, as well as at least two in Canada. Kam’s work, which continues to be heavily influenced by music, is in the permanent collection of the National Arts Council in Singapore and the Royal College of Music in London.
