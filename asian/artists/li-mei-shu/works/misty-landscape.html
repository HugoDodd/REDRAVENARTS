Untitled

Li Mei-shu

Watercolour on paper

35 x 16.5 cm

Signed Mei Shu

Inscriptions: Written "For Mother" verso.

Provenance: Horner Galleries, Sheffield (label on rear of frame); Purchased Lawrences of Bletchingly 10/14.

Artist Note: Early modernist Taiwanese artist. This watercolour work likely dates from the 1920s or 1930s, preceding his transition to oil painting.
