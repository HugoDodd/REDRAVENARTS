Mansoor Rahi

B. 1939

Artist Biography

Mansoor Rahi was born in what was then West Bengal. He graduated from the Government College of Art and Craft in Dhaka in 1963 but had his first solo exhibition in Dhaka as early as 1957. Rahi married another artist, since known as Hajra Mansoor, and the couple became a well-known artistic duo in Pakistani art circles. By the mid-1960s they had settled in Karachi, although later they moved to Islamabad. Mansoor Rahi developed a rather singular style based upon Cubism and, although most exhibitions of his work have been in Pakistani cities such as Karachi, Peshawar and Rawalpindi, he has also shown in Tokyo.
