Maqbool Fida Husain

1913–2011

Artist Biography

Maqbool Fida Husain (or M F Husain) is one of India’s most renowned artists from the 20th century, sometimes referred to as the Picasso of India. A founding member of the Bombay Progressive Artists Group he is best known for painting narratives in a cubist style. Husain’s work at times was controversial in his native India but its importance has never been disputed. It is held in major art galleries both globally and in India.
