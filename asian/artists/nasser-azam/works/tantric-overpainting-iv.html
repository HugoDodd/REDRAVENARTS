Tantric Overpainting IV

Nasser Azam

Acrylic, collage and mixed media on paper

118 × 84 cm

No signature.

Donated by artist to and sold on behalf of The Cambridge Mosque Project.

Tantric Overpainting III – same size, medium etc - was offered at the Special Yoga Centre’s 7th Annual Art Auction in May 2013 with a guide price of £6k. “Azam`s Tantric Overpainting uses a found source image and combines it with swirls and masse of pigment to create an explosive image, part of a series of works by Azam that explore the capacity of painted and printed marks to interact and blur the boundaries between abstraction and representation”. Solo show Saatchi Gallery June 2019.
