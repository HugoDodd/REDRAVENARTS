Natvar Bhavsar

b. 1934

Artist Biography

Natvar Bhavsar was born near Visnagar in Gujarat, India and studied art initially at the Seth CH College of Fine Art at Amdavad, where he taught from 1957-62, later gaining a masters in fine art in 1965 at the University of Pennsylvania. In India Bhavsar worked in a cubist style but in the United States he turned to abstract expressionism and colour field painting. Bhavsar married an American and settled in New York, but his work always retained a South Asian feel. He has had numerous exhibitions, having had a first solo show in 1970, mainly in the United States, Switzerland and in India. His work is held in public collections including the Metropolitan Museum of Art and the Guggenheim in New York, USA and the Museum of Contemporary Art and the National Gallery of Art in Australia. In 2011, a documentary on his life and works, The Poetics of Color: Natvar Bhavsar, a Painter's Journey was made by Sundaram Tagore.
