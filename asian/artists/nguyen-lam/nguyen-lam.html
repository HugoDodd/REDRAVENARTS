Nguyen Lam

b.1941

Artist Biography

Nguyen Lam was born in Can Tho, Vietnam. A painter in lacquers and oils who moved from expressionistic realism towards abstraction over the years, Lam was drafted into military service as a young man. He graduated from the National School of Fine Arts, Saigon in 1965 but had taken part in international exhibitions as early as 1961. He was a professor at the National School of Decoration and Fine Arts in Gia Dinh until the communist takeover of the south in 1975. Lam had regular solo shows in Saigon from 1963, including at the International Hall, L’Alliance Francaise and Vietnamese-American Associations. Internationally, his work was included in the Paris Biennale in 1961 and 1963 and major mixed exhibitions in Kuala Lumpur in 1963, New Delhi 1967 and New York in 1968. Since the 1960s his paintings have been exhibited in Tokyo, Washington, Singapore, Paris and Brussels. A notable recent mixed exhibition saw his work included in a 2015 London exhibition ‘Treasures of Vietnam’ organised by Hanoi Art House, UK. Lam’s paintings are in the permanent collections of the Vietnam National Museum of Fine Arts and Ho Chi Minh City Museum of Fine Arts.
