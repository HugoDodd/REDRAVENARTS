Nhek Dim

1934 – 1978

Artist Biography

Nhek Dim (anglicised as Nik Dim) was one of the first generation of modern Khmer artists. He studied at Phnom Penh’s Sala Rajana, or School of Art, under Japanese lecturer Suzuki and in the 1960s won a scholarship to Walt Disney studios to learn cartooning. Although an accomplished cartoonist, paintings were Dim’s first love and he went on in the 1960s and 1970s to become one of Cambodia’s most famous and successful artists. Dim opened a riverfront art gallery in Phnom Penh, selling his own work and that of a few relatives. Like most other artists, including his friend Sin Sisamouth, Dim perished during Khmer Rouge rule. His work is held in Cambodia’s National Museum in Phnom Penh and in 2001 a monograph was published in Khmer and English on Dim’s life (Lors Chinda Arts Publishers). Increasingly recognised as an important artist and cultural figure.
