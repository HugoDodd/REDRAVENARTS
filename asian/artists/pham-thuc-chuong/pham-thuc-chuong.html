Pham Thuc Chuong

1918 – 1983

Artist Biography

Pham Thuc Chuong was a Vietnamese artist who attended L’Ecole de Beaux Arts D’Indochine in Hanoi from 1933-38. He was one of six artists, including Vu Cao Dam and Le Pho, whose work helped illustrate a famous 1951 Vietnamese publication of the national epic Kieu. He moved to Europe and had a successful career, with many solo shows in France and Switzerland. One man exhibitions included Galerie Beno, Zurich in 1957, Wolfsberg Gallery, Zurich in 1961, Galerie Andre Weil in Paris in 1965, Galerie des Amis des Arts, Neuchatel in 1967 and Galerie ADC, Paris in 1971. His works often involved thick impasto and were painted in an impressionist style.
