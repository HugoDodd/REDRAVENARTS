Ram Yedekar

B. c. 1930s

Artist Biography

Ram Yedekar began working as an art director in the Indian film industry in the 1950s. Little is known of his early artistic training but his biography on IMDb notes that the government of Maharashtra contracted him to restore ancient wall paintings in caves near Mumbai, probably in the early 1960s. Oil paintings by Yedekar in that period suggest that, in addition to his set decoration work, he was a serious artist. As an art director Yedekar quickly gained success in Bollywood and in 1963 he won the Filmfare Best Art Director award for “Son of India”. His best known films include Sholay (1975) and, internationally, Gandhi (1982) and Octopussy (1983).
