Red Raven Arts / Asian / East Asian / Richard Lin (Lin Show Yu) / August 1968

August 1968

Richard Lin (Lin Show Yu)

Oil and aluminium on canvas

76 × 101 cm

Marlborough Gallery, London – where purchased by consignee (a Lady). Remains of Marlborough Gallery labels verso, where painting titled. Also titled in pencil verso, presumably in Lin’s hand. One label for Marlborough is for the Queen Elizabeth II’s maiden voyage (from Southampton to New York) exhibition in 1969.

Price for similar HK 2017 £200k. Lin being pushed by Bonhams and Art Asia Pacific Oct 15 said record £130k (since raised to £700k) showed that “high quality sells”. Same size 1969 Lin offered by Saltoun at Brit Art Fair 9/23 for £300k.
