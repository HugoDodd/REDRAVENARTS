Red Raven Arts / Asian Collection / East Asian / Richard Lin / August 1968

August 1968

Richard Lin

1968

Oil and aluminium on canvas

101 x 76 cm

Titled in pencil verso (presumably in the artist's hand)

Provenance: Marlborough Gallery, London (where originally acquired by the previous consignee); Private Collection; Purchased at Christie's (10/10).

Exhibition History & Labels: Features the remains of Marlborough Gallery documentation labels on the reverse canvas. Notably, one Marlborough exhibition label tracks the work to the historic 1969 Queen Elizabeth II Maiden Voyage Exhibition (Southampton to New York).
