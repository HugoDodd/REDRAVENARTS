Saleem Arif Quadri

B. 1949

Artist Biography

Saleem Quadri was born in Hyderabad, India and moved to England aged 17. He studied sculpture and painting at Birmingham College of Art 1969-72 and then at the Royal College of Art 1972-5. A sense of cultural dislocation and British and Italian government bursaries enabled Quadri to travel widely. Indian influences remain strong, but Quadri created his own symbolism, including birds, men and objects, which produced pictures often of great charm and subtlety.

Quadri has exhibited widely, highlights being solo shows at Art Heritage Gallery, New Delhi in 1984, Anderson O’Day Gallery, London in 1988, Scene Gallery, New York in 2000 and Shrishti Gallery, Hyderabad in 2006. Mixed exhibitions include the major show highlighting works by immigrant artists in Britain ‘The Other Story’ at the Hayward Gallery 1989-90, the Serpentine and Whitechapel galleries in London and others in Japan, India, France, Denmark, Italy and the USA. In 2010 his work was shown by the Aicon Gallery in a follow-up to ‘The Other Story’, and he was also included with four other Indian artists at the Shanti Panchal-curated ‘Paintings by Five’ exhibition at the Bhavan Centre, London. Government Art Collection, Arts Council, Tate Gallery and many regional galleries in Britain hold his work.
