Vanni Fucci and Monster Cacus

Saleem Arif Quadri

1984

Print and white paint

41 × 54 cm

Titled, signed and dated 1984, numbered 36/100.

Margaret and Charles Stockings collection (purchased as patrons). Unsold Bonhams NBS May ’22 at £1,000-1,500.

All in Arif’s hand in pencil recto. characters from Dante’s Inferno and Roman mythology. In 2010 Kensington exhibition Quadri was selling same size pieces for £3k each. Tate Modern 12/21 had entire wall of his works. Phillips/Grosvenor 7/25 sold wall of works for £35k.
