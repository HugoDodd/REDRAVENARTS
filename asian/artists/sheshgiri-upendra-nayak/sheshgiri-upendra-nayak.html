Sheshgiri Upendra Nayak

1912–1988

Artist Biography

Sheshgiri Upendra Nayak was born in 1912 in Gundbale, Karnataka. He joined the Sir J J School of Arts, Mumbai, with contemporaries such as K K Hebbar. Nayak used an impasto technique to build Indian landscapes using a muted, monochromatic impressionist palette. Although Nayak took part in group shows in Bombay in the 1940s, he began exhibiting his art more widely in the 1960s. He held several exhibitions at Gallery Chemould, Mumbai, which represented him. Nayak participated in group shows organised by Lintas and The Bombay Art Society. In 1965, he participated in the Sao Paulo Biennale, following a group show organised by Gallery Chemould in Brussels and Dusseldorf. Among his notable exhibitions were the ones held at the Taj Art Gallery, Bombay; Ashoka Art Gallery, New Delhi; and the Karnataka Chitrakala Parishad in Bangalore. His shows were attended by luminaries including the former Prime Minister of India, Indira Gandhi and the former Chief Minister of Karnataka, Shri Ramakrishna Hegde.

Nayak’s work is in the permanent collections of the Jehangir Nicholson foundation at the Museum-Mumbai, Birla Museum, Kolkata, the Taj Palace and Air India.
