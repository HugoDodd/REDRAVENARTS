Lee Shi-Chi

1938-2019

Artist Biography

Lee Shi-Chi was one of Taiwan’s most iconic modern masters. Born in Kinmen in 1938, he graduated in fine arts from the Taipei Normal School in 1958, immediately co-founding the Modern Graphic Art Association that same year. A relentlessly inventive force, Lee became a seminal member of the legendary Ton Fan Group, leading a generation that pushed the boundaries of postwar contemporary art across Asia.

Lee worked across an expansive variety of media, always balancing modernity with deep classical tradition, and bridging Eastern spiritual sensibilities with Western abstract formal systems. His graphics and canvases draw heavily on Chinese calligraphic movements, reimagined through clean geometric and lyrical abstraction. He held landmark solo exhibitions globally, showcasing in premier institutions across mainland China, Hong Kong, the United States, South Korea, Denmark, and Switzerland. In 2001, his monumental career was honored by a major cross-Straits cultural retrospective spanning Kinmen and Beijing.
