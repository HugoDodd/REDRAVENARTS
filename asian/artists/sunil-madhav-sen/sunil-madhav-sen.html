Sunil Madhav Sen

1910–1979

Artist Biography

Sunil Madhav Sen was born in Purulia, West Bengal. Although largely self-taught, visits to the studios of Tagore, Bose and others widened his artistic understanding. A founder member of the Calcutta Group, India’s first ‘modernist’ school of painters, Sen experimented in both Western and indigenous styles, eventually blending them into a vivid personal style. He had many solo shows in India and abroad, and was involved in group exhibitions in the UK, USA, Australia, Japan and Belgium. A major retrospective was held in 1993 at the Birla Academy of Art and Culture, Calcutta.
