Tsultem Niam-Osor

1923–2001

Artist Biography

Tsultem Niam-Osor (alternative spelling Tsultem Nyam Osory) was born in the Ara Khangai region of Mongolia. At the age of seven he began training in icon painting at a monastery in Ulan Bator and in 1940 he became a theatre artist and later the first artist to join the ‘Mongolkino’ cinema company. He then studied art in Russia at the Institute of Moscow from 1945-51. Niam-Osor went on to head the Union of Mongolian Artists from 1955-1990. A major Mongolian cultural figure, he won the Mongolian State Prize for art in 1970 and, in addition to his landscape and portrait painting, wrote a number of books on Mongolian art and art history. He was a key figure in setting up the Ulaan Bataar Museum of Fine Art in the 1960s and the Mongolian National Museum of Modern Art in 1989 and a monograph on his paintings from 1955 to 1997 was published in 2005. A 1955 steppe landscape is in the permanent collection of the Mongolian National Art Gallery.
