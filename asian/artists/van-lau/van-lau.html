Van Lau

b. 1933

Artist Biography

Van Lau was born in Guangdong, China and graduated in Fine Arts in 1959 at Taiwan Normal University. He had a first solo exhibition at the Hong Kong City Hall in 1964 and in 1965 obtained a scholarship to visit the United States. A further solo show of his sculptures followed in 1968 at LUZ Gallery, Manila and he was awarded a medal by the National Academy of Design, New York in 1970. His work was included in ‘Art Now. Hong Kong’ in 1971 at the City Museum & Art Gallery in Hong Kong and in a travelling exhibition of the same name that showed later in the year at the Commonwealth Institute in London and Edinburgh and at municipal art galleries in Manchester and Bristol. An exhibition of his work was also sponsored by the Hong Kong Arts Centre in 1979 and in 1984, he organized the first Hong Kong Open-Air Sculptures Exhibition. A major exhibition, 'The Art of Van Lau' was presented by the Hong Kong Museum of Art in 1987. 'The Exhibition of Sculpture by Van Lau' was shown at the Hsiung Shih Gallery in Taipei in 1992, which then transferred to the Sichuan Gallery. Van Lau lectured at The Chinese University of Hong Kong from 1973-75 and at the University of Hong Kong from 1984. He has a number of prominent public sculptures in Hong Kong and also has many of his artworks in the National Art Museum of China in Beijing. Van Lau is now the Chairman of the Hong Kong Artists Association and the President of the Hong Kong Sculptors Association.
