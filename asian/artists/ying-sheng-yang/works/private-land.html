Private Land

Ying Sheng Yang

1990

Oil on canvas

61 × 76 cm

Titled, signed, dated 1990 and marked London verso.

Exhibited by British Council in 1990. Image published in the box set Contemporary British Painting 1990 produced by the Royal College of Art for the British Council.

Royal College of Art stamp on stretcher. British Council label for the 1990 touring exhibition ‘3 Ways’. Seems also to be known sometimes as Ying Yang.
