Red Raven Arts was a London-based gallery that ran from 2005 to 2021 and specialised in modernist, expressionist and abstract paintings from the second half of the Twentieth Century.

Please note: we have closed the gallery down as a commercial enterprise but intend to continue to display paintings and information on artists on the site as a public information resource.

The paintings displayed are not for sale. Any extra information or corrections would be appreciated, so please email me at:
garethdodd@redravenarts.com
