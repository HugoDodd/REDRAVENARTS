Red Raven Arts was a London-based gallery specialising in modernist, expressionist, and abstract paintings from the second half of the Twentieth Century.

Closed as a commercial enterprise; maintained strictly as a public educational resources guide.

Paintings are part of a private collection and not for sale. Corrections or information: garethdodd@redravenarts.com.
