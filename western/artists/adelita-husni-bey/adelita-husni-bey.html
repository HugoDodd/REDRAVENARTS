Adelita Husni-Bey

b.1985

Artist Biography

Adelita Husni-Bey was born in Milan, Italy to parents of Tunisian heritage. She studied at Chelsea College of Arts, graduating in 2007, and then took an MA at Goldsmiths, University of London. She also in 2012-13 was a research fellow at the Whitney Museum of American Art and Husni-Bey is now New York based. Her work focuses on complex questions about gender, race and class using collectivist and informal pedagogical models within the framework of urban studies. She has worked in paint and collage amongst other media but is best known for photographic imagery and films of her organisation of group discussions challenging societal norms. A work of hers was shown at the Italian pavilion in 2017’s Venice biennale. Husni-Bey has a stellar exhibition record in numerous group shows from 2006 and solo shows from 2010 in Italy, the UK, Spain, Egypt, Holland the United States. A major exhibition was held in 2019 at the New Museum, New York and a large-scale video installation ‘Like a Flood’ was a prominent work at Sharjah Biennal 16 in 2025. The Museum of Modern Art in New York holds an example of one of her photographic works.
