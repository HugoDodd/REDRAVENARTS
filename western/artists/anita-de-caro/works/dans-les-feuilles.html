Dans les Feuilles

Anita de Caro

1960

Gouache/watercolour

45 × 14 cm

Signed, dated ‘60. Titled verso.

Collection of Bulgarian art critic, diplomat and academic Pierre Rouve (born Peter Christoff Ouvaliev). Came with a Hanover Gallery catalogue from a 1960 solo show of Anita de Caro for which Rouve wrote the introduction. This work is number 7 in that exhibition.

(in the leaves)
