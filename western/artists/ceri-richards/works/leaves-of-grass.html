Leaves of Grass

Ceri Richards

1968

Oil on canvas

35 × 25 cm

Signed, dated ’68. Titled and signed and titled again by Richards on label verso.

W&W £1500 hammer Dec 12 after unsold est. £3-5k Sep 12. According to W&W it was from the collection of Dr John Birch. Also sold Christies Mar 1997 at £1100 hammer (£1200-1800 est.), which is presumably where Birch bought it. A mixed media study for the painting went unsold at Sotheby’s est. £1500-2000 in 1987.

Also Marlborough Fine Art label with painting details. Catalogue No. 12. Probably named after Walt Whitman’s poetry collection of the same name. Illustrated on page 17 of the October 1970 Marlborough catalogue to accompany the gallery’s Ceri Richards solo exhibition, where indeed given exhibition number 12.
