Harmattan landscape with Figures

Clifford Frith

Oil on canvas

61 × 122 cm

Signed.

Illustrated on page 122 (fig 3.27) in Chika Okeke-Agulu’s 2015 book “Post-Colonial Modernism”. Okeke-Agulu describes the painting as showing Frith experimenting with abstract pictorial language and says the painting was in the collection of Grant Waters. The Harmattan is a dry, dusty wind that blows over West Africa in the winter seasons.

Label verso gives title. Also marked Zaria Art School 1960/61 and catalogue number 8.
