Dornroschen’s dinner -party

Dorle Lindner

Oil on canvas

76 × 100 cm

Initialled with an L and signed verso.

Titled in letter attached to rear in which Linder says the work was painted c. 2014. Dornroschen translates as Sleeping Beauty. Lindner explains the meaning as “Where is the Witch?” or “the uninvited guest”.
