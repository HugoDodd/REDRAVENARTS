Family Tree

Edith Rimmington

Photo

24 × 34 cm

George Melly collection. Purchased Rosebery’s 12/08. £44.15

Attributed to Rimmington verso. Surrealist; snake, jetty, chain. This work was shown at the 2007 exhibition: George Melly: The Blind Swimmer - A Celebration of the Life of George Melly. East West Gallery, 8 Blenheim Crescent, London W11 1NN Family Tree is described as a photo collage in the 1985 touring exhibition ‘A Salute to British Surrealism 1930-1950’ where it was on loan from Emmy Bridgewater and inscribed bottom right. It was also reproduced as a private view card for the Minories, Colchester exhibition. Family Tree was also shown at “Drawing Surrealism” at the Morgan Library and Museum in New York in 2013, a show jointly organised with LACMA. Is ours a second photo from 1937 or a different version of the original?
