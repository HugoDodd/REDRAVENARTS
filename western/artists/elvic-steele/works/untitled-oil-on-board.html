Untitled

Elvic Steele

Oil on board

50 × 43 cm

Signed verso. Signed on rear by Cedric Morris ESA(?), so likely a Benton End work.

In Sworder’s 2014 dispersal of Steele’s estate, where titled ‘The artist at her easel’.

Self-portrait painting. Also artist address label for The Lodge, New Barn, Lindsell (near Dunmow in Essex).
