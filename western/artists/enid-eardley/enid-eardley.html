Enid Eardley

1916 – 1999

Artist Biography

Enid Margaret Eardley was a painter, notable for portraits, who studied at Central School of Arts and Crafts in 1939, then in the mid-1960s with Stanley Grimm and Steven Spurrier. She was a member of the Society of Women Artists, exhibiting there from 1966 through to 1991, and had a series of solo exhibitions including Alpine Gallery in 1981. She also showed regularly with the Royal Society of British Artists at the Mall Galleries. She lived in Beaconsfield and then later in Old Amersham, Bucks.
