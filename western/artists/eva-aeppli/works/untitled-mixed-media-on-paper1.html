Untitled

Eva Aeppli

Mixed media on paper

30 × 34 cm

Annotated printed card, illustrated in felt tip and signed.

Sotheby’s 7 Feb 2005 where hammered for £300 on £500-700 estimate. This sale was of the Jean-Yves Mock collection and the work consists of annotated cards of Aeppli’s painting ‘Les Chanteur’ 1960 sent to Mock by Aeppli.
