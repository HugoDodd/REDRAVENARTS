Eve de Negri

1919 – 1977

Artist Biography

Eve de Negri was born in Britain of Italian extraction. She combined painting with lecturing at Central St Martin’s School of Design in London, before emigrating to Ibadan in Nigeria’s Western Region in 1959. De Negri was to remain in Nigeria to the end of her life. She researched local clothing, costumes and body adornment, writing books and articles on these in the 1960s and 1970s that are still regarded as an important resource on the subject. She moved to Lagos and in the mid-1960s she was instrumental, working with artist Yusuf Grillo, in founding the Fashion Department at Yaba College of Technology. Grillo called de Negri “a very good painter from Britain (with) a flair for fashion design”. De Negri had showed at the Royal Academy in London in 1963 and in 1965 was the first woman to have a solo exhibition at the Commonwealth Institute in London, titled ‘Paintings of Nigeria’. There is a pathe news video of this exhibition. At the same time de Negri was given solo shows in Lagos, notably in 1965 at the Exhibition Centre in Marina, Lagos and then in 1966 when the Exhibition Centre moved to the Independence Building. One of de Negri’s drawings was also used in the UNICEF calendar of 1969. The Yusuf Grillo Museum in Lagos holds her work in its permanent collection.
