Green Indian Red

Frank Avray Wilson

Oil on board

56 × 76 cm

Title chalked on rear (note: missing gallery label was put on top of chalk title). c.1957/58?. Possible exhibition or auction number 62. (some similarities in terms of paint used to Configuration II 1958 (29x38cm, unsigned) at Hepworth Gallery, Wakefield. Also one painting titled Green: Red (number 202) shown in 1957 exhibition at the Redfern Gallery. + “composition red, yellow, green” 55x76 oil/masonite sold at Ritchie’s Ontario in 2003 is very much same shapes and style. Signed, date ’57, titled. Handy, for definitive proof this is a FAW, if can find an exhibited painting with the title.
