High Red

Frank Avray Wilson

1967

Oil on board

121 × 90 cm

Signed, dated ’67. Titled on sticker verso for Federation of British Artists exhibition in Pittsburgh, USA.

By descent from the artist’s heirs. The Carnegie Institute in Pittsburgh held the "Pittsburgh International Exhibition of Contemporary Painting and Sculpture, 1967" in January 1967 and "High Red" APPEARS to be part of the British offering via the Federation of British Artists.

(prob 36”x48” under frame). Also remains of label for Atkinson Art Gallery of Southport, so was probably in its spring exhibition in ’67 or ’68. F.A. Wilson written verso twice. Chalk number E1515 or E15/5.
