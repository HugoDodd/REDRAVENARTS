No. 4

Frank Avray Wilson

1959

Oil on board

92 × 122 cm

Signed, dated ’59.

Avray Wilson’s son (against wishes of father).

Abstract. Title verso. (ie 36”x48”). Marc Ottavi sticker. Image used in 1961 Avray Wilson book by Oliver Carson for Redfern Gallery, but incorrect sizing and date (1960) given. (Paisnel 2011 would sell c.£20k. Note, Paisnel’s prices rising – one unsigned painting 99x124cm was £16,500 in 2013 but £22k 2014) Lawrence says these Paris paintings were exhibited and unsold at the Galerie Fricker exhibition of 1961.
