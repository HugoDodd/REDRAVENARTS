Georgina Beier

From a local house clearance. Old framing, print probably from the 1960s. Chike immediately said this was a Georgina Beier. Google AI says it is a known piece by Beier seen at Theodore Bruce Auctioneers when selling the collection of Ellen Margaret Waugh (a PNG specialist).

Colourful figures and animals. Sticker on frame has exhibition number 41. Sheet 45x49cm (H x W).
