L’Hydre

Germaine Richier

1954

Etching with aquatint on paper

54 × 38 cm

Not signed.

Image created 1954-55, published 1961. this aquatint was published in an edition of 125 in 1961 with a stamped signature bottom right (see Tate Modern). This had an image size of 28x38cm. Our example is the same size as that in MOMA New York which is unsigned and described by the museum as being a proof taken before the print run. The printer is said to be unknown and the publisher Schweizerische Graphische Gesellschaft of Zurich. A signed undated artist proof 52x66cm version of L’Hydra was exhibited at Richier’s solo exhibition at the Centre Pompidou in Paris in 2023, described as an embossed etching and aquatint. This had been purchased by the Musee d’Art Moderne de la Ville de Paris in 1965. It is illustrated in the book ‘Germaine Richier’ edited by Ariane Coulandre published in 2023, page 182. Sheet 50x64cm (H x W).
