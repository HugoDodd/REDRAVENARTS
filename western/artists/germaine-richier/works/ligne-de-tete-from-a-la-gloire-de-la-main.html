Ligne de Tete from A la Gloire de la Main

Germaine Richier

Etching and drypoint on paper

18 × 12 cm

Not signed.

sheets . The front sheet has just the image, the front of the rear sheet is printed 10 and Germaine Richier, whilst the rear is printed Ligne de Tete and Eau Forte (i.e. an etching). It is possible that the crayon writing is in Richier’s handwriting. Other size 22x28cm (H x W).
