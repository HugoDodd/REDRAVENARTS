Red Raven Arts / British & European / Ithell Colquhoun / Bikini

Bikini

Ithell Colquhoun

1966

Acrylic on paper on board

109 × 71 cm

Signed with monogramme.

Exhibited Kunstamt Wilmersdorf, Berlin 1969, Bristol Arts Centre, Bristol 1970 (illustrated in b/w in the catalogue). Purchased David Lay, Penzance 06/04. Previously sold Biddle & Webb, Birmingham in 1998 for £780 hammer.

Known in official catalogue as ‘Nuclear Explosion – Bikini’. Bikini is the counterpart of the top part of the 1952 work Atomic Psychosis (process of decalcomania produces a negative counterpart). Cost £204 + £70 frame = £274.
