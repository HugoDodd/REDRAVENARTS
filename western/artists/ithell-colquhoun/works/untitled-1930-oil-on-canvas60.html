Untitled

Ithell Colquhoun

1930

Oil on canvas

35 × 51 cm

Signed, dated 1930.

Offered at Sotheby’s in 1983 at £300-500 and unsold. Sotheby’s catalogued the work’s provenance as the artist.

Self-portrait painted in 1930 whilst at Slade School, on old canvas of military man. Sticker Number 1 on rear, plus label for Stone Cross Cottage address. (failed to sell at Bonhams in 2006, estimated £2-3k)
