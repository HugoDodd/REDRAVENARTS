Untitled

Ithell Colquhoun

Gouache

38 × 38 cm

Unsigned.

Bought by Frank Avray Wilson’s daughter Wendy Wilson-Holzbog at Bonhams’ English and Continental Pictures sale on 19/10/88. Lot 137, hammer £55 on a £10-20 estimate. The name Ithell Colquhoun is handwritten on the receipt. The Getty art library sent me a copy of the Bonhams entry: Attributed to Ethel Colohoun: Floral Design, watercolour, signed in pencil on reverse, 14.5 x 15 ins (36x38cms) £10/20

Circle of life. Marked Ithell Colquhoun? verso. The image fits well with what Amy Hale writes about Colquhoun in her 2020 book Genius of the Fern Loved Gully, notably in the use of tesseract colours, spirituality, female figures and geometry.
