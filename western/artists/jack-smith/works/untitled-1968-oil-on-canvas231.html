Untitled

Jack Smith

1968

Oil on canvas

106 × 106 cm

Signed, titled and dated ’68 verso.

Unsold March 2011 at Christies estimated £6-8k and December 2011 estimated £3-5k – got for £1800. Previously sold at Sotheby’s London 6 December 2000, lot 161. £1900 hammer. Labels on backboard suggest also sold at Phillips May 1973 and that it might have one day belonged to the World Wildlife Fund.

Sothebys 9/13 asking £45k each for 106x106cm Written works from 1965 and 1967. Unsold.
