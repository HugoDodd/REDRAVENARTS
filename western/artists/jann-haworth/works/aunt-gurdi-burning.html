Aunt Gurdi Burning

Jann Haworth

1995

Oil on silk mounted on metal screens

183 × 122 cm

Titled and signed on middle stretcher where dated Sept 1995. Titled verso.

Chelsea and Westminster Hospital, which bought the artwork in 1995 from Gimpel Fils Gallery, London. Aunt Gurdie was a rather wild aunt of Jann Haworth who fell into alcoholism and died in her bed when her cigarette set fire to it. C & W originally intended to put I the painting near their burns unit but thought better of it later. The painting appears in a Tom Cruise hospital scene in Kubrick’s 1999 movie Eyes Wide Shut.

(in three parts).
