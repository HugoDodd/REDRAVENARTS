Rock forms at Tafraout

Jo Jones

Oil on canvas

60 × 82 cm

Signed.

Exhibition No 3 inscribed on rear. (Tafraout is in NW Morocco). This painting was exhibit No.15 in the 1974 O’Hana Gallery solo show. It was also probably No 11 in 1968 exhibition at same gallery.
