Melted Snow on the V&A roof

John Bratby RA

Oil on board

76 × 111 cm

Signed.

Julian Hartnoll, where was for sale at £9,500 in 2003. Julian dates it to c.1952-53, although Bratby later inscribed it as 1954 on the stretcher. Bratby was at the RCA 1951-4. The title is Hartnoll’s.
