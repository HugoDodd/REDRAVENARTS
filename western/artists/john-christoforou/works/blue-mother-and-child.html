Blue Mother and Child

John Christoforou

1958

Oil on board

90 × 125 cm

Signed, dated 1958. Titled and dated verso.

Purchased by a Mr Howard from Gallery One. Exhibited by Grosvenor Gallery at Frieze Masters 2023. Grosvenor Gallery Frieze Masters 2023 label verso. Sold at Bonhams by Howard’s brother. Howard was a major supporter of Christoforou and Souza.

Robin Jared Stanley Howard was a British philanthropist, art collector, dance patron and founder of The Place who promoted modern dance in England. Howard, grandson of Prime Minister Stanley Baldwin, studied at Eton and then – after war service in the Scots Guards where he lost both legs –took Law at Trinity College, Cambridge. He went into business before devoting himself full-time to patronage of modern dance from 1963. Although modern dance was his first love, Howard was a passionate supporter of Victor Musgrave’s Gallery One and he amassed a major collection of works by John Christoforou, F N Souza and others. Following his death his family have gradually sold off the collection, largely through Bonhams.
