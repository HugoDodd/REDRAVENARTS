Mother and Child

John Christoforou

Gouache

56 × 76 cm

Not signed. Titled in Christoforou’s hand verso.

No. 11 on old backboard. Called ‘Lovers’ by JP. Gallery One receipt from March ’57 shows the sale of this to a Mrs M. Phillips. All Christoforou’s bought from Ms Pruskin were either purchased by her from Timothy Prus in the mid-1980s (who had bought from Musgrave’s wife Monica Kinley) or directly from Kinley. The same applies to some such as Blue Head bought by me at Bonhams or Christies in earlier years.
