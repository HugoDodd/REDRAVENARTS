Musician

John Christoforou

1956

Oil on board

122 × 94 cm

Signed. Titled on rear and dated 1956.

Piccadilly Gallery, Cork Street Dec 1971, where bought by Surrey County Council. This work formed part of Surrey County Council’s Art Loan Collection until it was disbanded in 2004. Purchased CSK 7/04.
