Red Raven Arts / British & European / John Melville / Nude

Nude

John Melville

1957

Oil on board

120 × 90 cm

Signed twice verso and marked Gale & Co.. Also signed, titled and dated on 1957 John Moores Exhibition label verso.

Exhibited at John Moores, Liverpool. Believed to have been exhibited at Melville’s 1967 solo exhibition at the RBSA – as ‘Nude against Blue Background’. Purchased May 2003 from Bonhams, Knightsbridge.

Another label for R. Jackson & Sones, Exhibition Agents. Gale & Co is a famous Birmingham framers (est. 1845). Cost £1246 + £90 clean = £1336 + frame.
