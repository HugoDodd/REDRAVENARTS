Portrait of Betty

John Melville

1944

Oil on canvas

35 × 40 cm

Signed. Titled and dated 1944 on artist label verso, where numbered 19.

Exhibited at City of Birmingham Museum and Art Gallery’s 1945 ‘An Exhibition of Paintings and Sculpture by Four Birmingham Artists’.

Label says ‘Mrs John Melville, 33 Hagley Road, Edgebaston, B’ham 16’.
