John Stezaker

b.1949

Artist Biography

John Stezaker, artist in photomontage, was born in Worcester. He studied at Slade School of Fine Art 1967-73. While still a student he began to exhibit, having a solo show at Sigi Krauss Gallery in 1970. Another followed at Nigel Greenwood Inc in 1972, the start of a long association with that gallery. The 1970s saw Stezaker’s reputation grow, with one-man shows at Museum of Modern Art in Oxford, Photographers’ Gallery London, the Ikon Gallery Birmingham and City Museum, Southampton, as well as several in Europe. Group shows included the Hayward Gallery 1972, Paris Biennale in 1973 and many others. From 1975 Stezaker taught at St Martin’s School of Art. He later joined the Royal College of Art where he is now a senior lecturer. Highly regarded as an avant-garde conceptual artist, Stezaker has an international reputation. Contemporary galleries that sell his work include David Risley Gallery and The Approach Gallery. He held his first solo show in New York at White Columns in Feb-March 2006 and his work was chosen in the Tate’s 2006 Triennial show of New British Art curated by Beatrix Ruf of the Kunsthalle, Zurich. 2007 and 2008 saw solo exhibitions at the London Galleries Karsten Schubert and The Approach. In 2009 Stezaker had a solo show at Friedrich Petzel Gallery, New York in Feb-Mar 09, receiving excellent reviews. Another at Petzel Jan-Feb 11. Whitechapel Art Gallery solo show Jan-Mar 11. Tel Aviv Museum of Art Jan-May 2013, Petzel in New York May-Jun 2013. May-July 2015 John Stezaker: Filmworks at De La Warr Pavilion, Bexhill-on-Sea. Tate Gallery, Arts Council, the Museum of Modern Art in New York and other major institutions hold examples of his work.
