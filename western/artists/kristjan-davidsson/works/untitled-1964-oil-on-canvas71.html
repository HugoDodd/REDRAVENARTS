Untitled

Kristjan Davidsson

1964

Oil on canvas

60 × 65 cm

Signed and dated ’64. Also signed again and dated ’64 verso.

Possibly from the Demarco Gallery, Edinburgh – Davidsson had mixed shows there at Christmas ’65 and March ’66 and a solo exhibition there in 1966.

Dedication verso “to Mr R. Jordan with compliments from Mr Ingvarsson”. Marked ‘Ljosm’ on stretcher.
