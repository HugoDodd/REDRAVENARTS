Nature Morte aux Poires

Leon Zack

Oil on canvas

40 × 33 cm

Signed.

Remains of label verso giving title and suggesting purchased from Galerie Louis Manteau (a Brussels gallery at which Zack showed in a New Humanists group exhibition in 1931). Purchased Burstow & Hewett 2/05.

Painting looks later than 1931 – possibly late 1930s/mid-1940s. Cost £430 + £35 refurb = £465.
