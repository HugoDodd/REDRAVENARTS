Android

Magda Cordell

1958

Oil on canvas

102 × 153 cm

Signed, titled and dated 1958 verso.

Note says Frankel verso. Owner: Mrs Frankel, 43 Manor Way, Blackheath SE3 9XG (son Jerry 07983 069705). Mrs Frankel won this at a raffle at the ICA in 1958 where she paid £15 for a ticket and then got a choice of painting – which had been donated by the artists for fundraising. See the ICA leaflet. Same size ‘Doppler Type 2’ from 1957 being sold by Jack Wakefield 9/23 at Brit Art fair for £125k. Bonhams asking £100k for same size but much worse one 2/26.
