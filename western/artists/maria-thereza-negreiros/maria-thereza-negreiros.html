Maria Thereza Negreiros

1930 –

Artist Biography

Maria Thereza Negreiros was born in Maues, Amazonia State, Brazil and studied art at the National School of Fine Art in Rio de Janeiro. She married a Colombian architect and moved with him to Cali, Colombia where they settled. Negreidos had a first solo exhibition in 1961, initially painting oils on canvas inspired by Andes landscapes in a colourful and expressionist abstract style. Whilst she later experimented with a wide variety of artistic media she was eventually to return to painting in oils, concentrating on an epic Amazonian series. Negreiros became an important and influential artist in Colombia, exhibiting at prestigious venues across the country on a regular basis. She also represented Colombia at the 1963 VII Sao Paulo Biennal. Her work was shown across the Americas, including in Cuba, Argentina, Brazil, Guatemala, the United States and Canada. Important one-man exhibitions included 1963 at the Pan American Union, Washington, United States, 1967 at Gallery IBEU in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, a 40-year retrospective in 2002 in Cali, Colombia and a major show in 2012 at the Frost Art Museum, Miami in the United States. She also was represented in mixed shows in Madrid, London, Paris and Tokyo. Many public collections in Colombia hold her work, the best known being the Museum of Modern Art in Bogota.
