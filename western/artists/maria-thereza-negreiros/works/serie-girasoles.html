Serie: Girasoles

Maria Thereza Negreiros

1962

Oil on canvas

108 × 93 cm

Signed and dated ‘62.

On verso marked ‘Serie: Girasoles. Negreiros 1962’. Girasoles translates as sunflower. This was one of her most important early abstract series and came after she grew a field of sunflowers and then began to take inspiration from their colours.
