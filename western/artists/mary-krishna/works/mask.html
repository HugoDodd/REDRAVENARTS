Mask

Mary Krishna (Indumati Roop Krishna)

Oil on silk

20 × 23 cm

Signed Indumati Roop Krishna.

Almost certainly the ‘Mask’ from the 1978 retrospective at Belgrave Gallery where it is described as being watercolour on silk.

Title, media and date 1936 marked verso. Described as gouache on canvas by High Road auctions at a previous sale.
