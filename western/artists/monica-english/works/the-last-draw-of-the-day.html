The Last Draw of the Day

Monica English

1965

Oil pastels on paper

49 × 60 cm

Titled, artist details and dated 1965 on backboard, all in English’s hand.

Second picture of two prancing horses verso. (the three works from Clifford Cross were from the estate of a Wisbech antiques dealer, Peter Crofts, who frequented the Fermoy Gallery).
