Townscape

Norman Ackroyd

1963

Oil on canvas

71 × 101 cm

Signed. Dated ‘63, titled and signed again verso.

This work was given in 2006 by Ackroyd to a builder who carried out work on his studio.

Plus address details and original price £42. The scene is almost certainly of Leeds, being very similar to one entitled "St Mary's, Leeds" in a 1962 etching. (Ackroyd curating RA Summer Exhib 2013 – showed a mid-sized landscape of the Stour Estuary at £48k)
