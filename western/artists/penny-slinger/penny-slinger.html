Penny Slinger

b.1947

Artist Biography

Tantric multimedia artist Penny Slinger is best known for her sacred erotic art and her Caribbean/Amerindian work. She studied at Farnham and then Chelsea Colleges of Art, graduating in 1969, and was heavily influenced by Surrealism, notably by Max Ernst. After college her work was included in an ICA exhibition but in 1979 she moved to the West Indies and rather slipped from the UK art world’s radar. In recent years her reputation as a surrealist and feminist artist has grown. Slinger’s collages, often involving photo images of her own body, were included in 2009’s ‘Angels of Anarchy’ exhibition on female Surrealists at Manchester Art Gallery and in the Tate St Ives exhibition in 2009/10 ‘The Dark Monarch’. Her works were also included in a 2015 European touring show (Hamburg etc) entitled ‘The Feminist Avant-Garde of the 1970s’. Riflemaker in London gave her a solo show in 2012 ‘Hear What I Say’ focusing on photo-collages from 1971-1977. Richard Saltoun solo show 2019.
