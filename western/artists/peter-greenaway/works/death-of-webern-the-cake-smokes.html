Death of Webern: The Cake Smokes

Peter Greenaway

1988

Pencil on card

81 × 112 cm

Signed, dated 1988. Titled recto plus verso on Nicole Klagsbrun, New York gallery label.

Also Luper Productions label plus explanation in French of the painting. This painting is a “speculative staging of the first act” of a putative ten act Greenaway opera ‘The Death of Webern and Others’. Michael Nyman was to have provided the music. Full page photo plus explanation in 1990 publication Papers by Greenaway.
