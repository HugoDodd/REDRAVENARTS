Untitled

Susanne Wenger (Adunni Olorisha)

Linocut

50 × 76 cm

Unsigned, c.1960s.

Submitted to the auction house by a small time local art dealer as a West African print from the 1960s but no further provenance. Looks to be Iwin series c.1960. Frame is battered, old, untouched. These are worth c.£2k at auction in Nigeria.

Numbered 8838 and 13 on a sticker on old frame. Exhibited by Grosvenor Gallery at Frieze Masters 2023. (Ko Gallery ’25 selling similar screenprint $7k)
