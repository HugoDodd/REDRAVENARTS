Angry Black

Willy Tirr

1964

Acrylic on canvas

122 × 183 cm

Signed verso. Titled verso on remains of exhibition label.

The artist’s family. Address means the painting is post-1955.

Address for No.1 Belvedere View. Illustrated as Figure 21 on page 40 in David Manson’s 2010 book ‘Willy Tirr Figure in a Landscape’. Possibly exhibited as ‘Angry Blue and Black’ at SE62 – Bradford’s Cartwight Hall Spring Exhibition in 1962 – priced £120. But an ‘Angry’ of the right size – said to be painted in 1964 – was exhibited at his 1979 retrospective at the Scott Gallery, University of Lancaster.
